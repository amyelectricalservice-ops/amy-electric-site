Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency electrical service in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — AMY Electric provides 24-hour emergency electrical service 7 days a week throughout Los Angeles and the San Fernando Valley, including weekends and holidays. Call (818) 302-5614 any time to reach a licensed electrician who can assess the situation and dispatch the nearest available crew, typically within 60-90 minutes.
What qualifies as an electrical emergency? ▾
Any situation with an immediate risk of fire, shock, or damage: sparking or smoking panels, burning smells from outlets, exposed live wires, a breaker that won't stay reset, partial power outage affecting only your home, or downed service lines. If you're unsure, call — we'll help you assess it over the phone. For active fire or downed power lines, call 911 first.
How fast can you reach me in Los Angeles? ▾
For most Los Angeles addresses we arrive in 60-90 minutes. Distant areas such as Santa Monica or Calabasas may take 90-120 minutes depending on traffic. For life-threatening situations we coordinate with the LAFD and can often be on site within 30-45 minutes.
Do you handle emergency panel repairs and power restoration? ▾
Yes. We diagnose and repair sparking panels, failed breakers, and partial outages, and we perform temporary solutions to safely restore power until a permanent repair can be completed. Our trucks carry parts for most common emergencies so we can resolve them on the first visit.
