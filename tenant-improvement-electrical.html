Frequently Asked Questions

TI Electrical FAQ

What electrical work is included in a tenant improvement? +

Typical TI electrical includes lighting layout and installation, power distribution, data/telecom wiring, HVAC control wiring, fire alarm modifications, and code-compliant emergency lighting.

How much does tenant improvement electrical cost? +

Basic office buildouts run $15-$25 per square foot. Medical facilities and restaurants run $25-$40 per square foot. We provide detailed proposals based on your TI plans.

Do you work with general contractors? +

Yes. We regularly work as a subcontractor under general contractors on TI projects. We coordinate on scheduling and ensure our work integrates with other trades.

How long does TI electrical work take? +

A typical 2,000-5,000 sq ft office TI takes 2-4 weeks for electrical work. Medical facilities and restaurants may take longer.

Can you work in occupied buildings? +

Yes. We minimize disruption with noise control, dust containment, and flexible scheduling. We can work evenings and weekends when needed.
