Los Angeles Homes and Electrical Wiring

Los Angeles has one of the oldest housing stocks in California. Thousands of homes built before 1950 still have original knob-and-tube or cloth-wired circuits. The city's diverse architecture — from Craftsman bungalows in Angelino Heights to mid-century ranches in the Valley — all face similar aging-wire challenges.

Our Process

How We Rewire Los Angeles Homes

01

Inspection

We use thermal imaging to identify all active knob-and-tube and cloth-wired circuits in your Los Angeles home.

02

Permits

We pull the electrical permit with Los Angeles Building Department and create a circuit map.

03

Rewire

New Romex NM-B is routed through attic, crawl spaces, and wall cavities. Old wiring is disconnected and removed.

04

Inspection

Building inspector verifies the work. You receive a signed permit and inspection certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Rewiring FAQ for Los Angeles

How much does whole-home rewiring cost in Los Angeles? +

Rewiring costs depend on home size, accessibility, and wiring condition. A typical Los Angeles home runs $8,000-$18,000 for a full rewire. Partial rewiring of high-risk areas (kitchen, bath, attic) starts around $3,000-$6,000. We provide free on-site estimates with a written scope of work.

Do you serve all of Los Angeles? +

Yes, we serve all neighborhoods in Los Angeles including Hollywood, Silver Lake, Echo Park. We're based in nearby Winnetka and can typically schedule within 2-3 business days.

How long does rewiring take in Los Angeles? +

A full rewire typically takes 3-7 days depending on home size and attic/crawl space accessibility. Partial rewiring of specific areas takes 1-2 days. We work efficiently to minimize disruption.

Do I need a permit for rewiring in Los Angeles? +

Yes, Los Angeles requires an electrical permit for any rewire work. Our permit fee is included in your estimate. We handle the entire permitting process and ensure everything passes inspection.

Will rewiring increase my home value in Los Angeles? +

Yes. Modern wiring is a major selling point in Los Angeles. Buyers and inspectors expect grounded, code-compliant electrical systems. Homes with updated wiring sell faster and at higher prices.
