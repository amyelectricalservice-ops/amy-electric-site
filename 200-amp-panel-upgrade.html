Why 200 Amps?

Is Your Panel Keeping Up With Your Home?

Most homes built before 2000 have 100-amp service. That was enough for lights and a few appliances. Today's homes need more:

⚡ EV Charger Ready

Level 2 EV chargers draw 30-50 amps. A 200-amp panel gives you capacity for charging plus everything else.

❄️ Heat Pump HVAC

Modern heat pump systems require dedicated 240V circuits. A 200-amp panel handles the load safely.

🏠 Home Value

A 200-amp panel is expected by buyers and inspectors. Upgrading before selling can prevent deal-killing repairs.

📋 Code Compliance

NEC 2020 requires 60-amp minimum service for new construction. Upgrading brings older homes closer to current code.

Our Process

How We Upgrade Your Panel

01

Load Calculation

We perform a NEC Article 220 load calculation to confirm you need 200 amps. Some homes can add capacity without a full upgrade.

02

Permits

We pull the electrical permit with LADBS. This includes the service entrance, panel, and grounding work.

03

Shut Down & Swap

We coordinate with LADWP for service disconnect, remove the old panel, install the new 200A panel, and reconnect.

04

Inspection & Power-On

LADBS inspects the installation. Once approved, we coordinate with LADWP to restore service.

Frequently Asked Questions

200 Amp Panel Upgrade FAQ

How much does a 200 amp panel upgrade cost in Los Angeles? +

A 200-amp panel upgrade typically runs $2,500-$5,000 depending on your existing service, panel location, and whether the meter base and weatherhead also need replacement. All estimates include permits and LADBS inspection.

Do I need a 200 amp panel? +

If you have a 100-amp panel and are adding an EV charger, heat pump HVAC, or major appliances, you likely need a 200-amp upgrade. We perform a load calculation during your free estimate to confirm.

How long does a panel upgrade take? +

Most 200-amp panel upgrades take 1-2 days. You will be without power for 4-8 hours during the panel swap. We schedule a same-day inspection when possible.

Will I need to upgrade my meter base? +

If your existing meter base is rated for 100 amps or is damaged/corroded, it must be upgraded to handle 200 amps. We inspect the meter base during the estimate and include it in the quote if needed.

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade? +

Yes. Los Angeles requires an electrical permit for any panel upgrade. Our permit fee is included in your estimate. We handle the entire permitting process with LADBS.
