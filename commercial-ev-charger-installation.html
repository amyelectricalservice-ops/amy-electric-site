Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial EV Charging FAQ

How many EV chargers can I install at my commercial property? +

This depends on your electrical service capacity. A typical 200A panel can support 2-4 Level 2 chargers with load management. Larger commercial services (400A+) can support 10+ stations. We perform a load calculation to determine your capacity.

Do I need EVITP certification for commercial charging? +

EVITP certification is required in many jurisdictions for commercial EV charging installation. Our team holds EVITP certification #4051604 and is qualified to install all major commercial charging brands.

What rebates are available for commercial EV charging in LA? +

LADWP offers rebates for commercial EV charging infrastructure. The federal Alternative Fuel Vehicle Refueling Property Credit (30C) covers up to 30% of installation costs. We help you navigate all available incentives.

How long does commercial EV charger installation take? +

A single Level 2 charger installation takes 1-2 days. Multi-station projects with load management take 3-7 days depending on the number of stations and electrical work required.

Can I add EV charging without upgrading my electrical panel? +

In many cases, yes. Load management systems dynamically distribute available power across multiple chargers, preventing demand spikes. This allows you to install more chargers than your panel's rated capacity would normally support.
