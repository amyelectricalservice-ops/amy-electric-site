Why Troubleshooting?

Same-Day Electrical Diagnostics in Los Angeles

Electrical problems rarely announce themselves clearly. A dead outlet might be a tripped GFCI upstream, a broken device, or an open wire. A tripping breaker could be an overload, a short, or a failing breaker. AMY Electric provides same-day diagnostic service to find the root cause — not just treat the symptom.

Common Problems

Electrical Problems We Diagnose and Fix

🔌 Dead Outlets

Tripped GFCI upstream, broken device, or open wire. We trace the cause and repair it, often in the same visit.

⚡ Tripping Breakers

Overloaded circuit, short, ground fault, or failing breaker. We perform a load test and inspection to find the root cause.

🔥 Burning Smell

Overheating, loose connection, or arcing — a fire hazard. Turn off the affected circuit and call the same day.

💡 Flickering Lights

Loose bulb, switch, or service connection. We trace from the fixture to the panel to find the cause.

🏠 Partial Power Loss

Tripped breaker, failed GFCI, or loose neutral. We diagnose and restore power safely.

⚡ Panel/Feeder Issues

Overheating, corrosion, or failed breakers. We assess panel condition and recommend repair or replacement.

Our Process

  1. Diagnostic visit. We test, inspect, and trace the root cause, then provide a written quote.
  2. Repair plan. We explain the cause, the fix, and the cost before any work begins.

Permits & Code

Permit Considerations for Electrical Troubleshooting in LA

  • Minor repairs permit-exempt. Replacing a device, switch, or fixture does not require a permit.
  • Permit for significant work. Panel, feeder, or circuit work requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200-$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on panel or feeder work.

LA Considerations

Los Angeles Electrical Issues We See Most Often

  • Aging panels. Zinsco, Federal Pacific, and Pushmatic panels in 1960s-1980s homes fail to trip properly.
  • Aluminum wiring. Common in 1965-1978 construction; connections loosen and overheat.
  • Knob-and-tube. Pre-1950s wiring with no ground wire, often buried under insulation.
  • Overloaded circuits. Older homes weren't wired for modern loads (EV chargers, AC, home offices).
  • GFCI/AFCI gaps. Missing protection in kitchens, baths, garages, and outdoor areas.

Electrical Troubleshooting FAQ

How much does electrical troubleshooting cost in Los Angeles? +

Electrical troubleshooting in Los Angeles costs $150 to $350 for a diagnostic service call, waived if repair is booked. Minor repairs run $150 to $300, moderate repairs $300 to $800, and major repairs $800 to $1,500+. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after diagnosis.

Why is my outlet dead? +

A dead outlet can be caused by a tripped GFCI upstream, a broken device, or an open wire. AMY Electric traces the cause and repairs it, often in the same visit.

Why does my breaker keep tripping? +

A tripping breaker usually indicates an overloaded circuit, short circuit, ground fault, or failing breaker. AMY Electric performs a load test and inspection to find the root cause.

Is a burning smell an emergency? +

Yes. A burning smell from an outlet, switch, or panel indicates overheating or arcing — a fire hazard. Turn off the affected circuit and call a licensed electrician the same day.

Do I need a permit for electrical repair? +

Minor repairs (device replacement) are usually permit-exempt. Panel, feeder, or circuit work requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection. AMY Electric files any required permits and attends the inspection.
