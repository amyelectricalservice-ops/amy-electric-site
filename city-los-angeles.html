Electrician in Los Angeles — FAQ

Do you serve Los Angeles for same-day repairs? ▾
Yes — we provide 24/7 emergency dispatch for urgent electrical issues. Call (818) 302-5614 anytime, day or night. We prioritize emergencies including power outages affecting only your home, sparking or smoking panels, exposed wires, and any situation involving water near electricity. Our electricians carry diagnostic equipment and common replacement parts to handle most emergencies on the first visit. When availability allows, we dispatch same-day for non-emergency repairs as well.
Who handles permits for electrical work in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — we handle all permitting and inspections as part of every installation. We manage the entire process: permit applications with LADBS (Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety), plan review submissions, coordination with LADWP for service upgrades, scheduling inspections, and obtaining the final Certificate of Completion. The permit process typically takes 3-10 business days, depending on the scope of work. Permitting ensures code compliance and protects your insurance.
Do you install EV chargers in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — we regularly install Level 2 EV chargers in Los Angeles for Tesla, Rivian, ChargePoint, and all other EV brands. EVITP-certified, permits handled, free estimates.
