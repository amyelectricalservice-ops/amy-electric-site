Ceiling Fan Installation Process

1
Assessment & Quote

Tell us about the room, ceiling height, and fan type desired. We check joist location and provide a firm quote.

2
Support Boxing (if needed)

If no existing fan box, we install a fan-rated brace between joists to safely support the fan's weight and torque.

Wiring & Mounting

Run new wiring or connect to existing circuit. Securely mount the fan bracket and hang the fan assembly.

4
Blade Installation & Balancing

Attach blades, install light kit (if applicable), and balance the fan to eliminate wobble and noise.

Testing & Walkthrough

Test all speeds, light kit, and any smart features. Show you operation and cleanup before we leave.

    Ceiling Fan FAQ

    Do I need a permit to install a ceiling fan in Los Angeles? ▾
    If you're replacing an existing fan with a similar box and wiring, typically no permit is needed. If we're installing a new box, running new wiring, or working in an attic, a permit is usually required. We advise you during the estimate.

    Can my ceiling support a fan? ▾
    We inspect the joists and existing box (if any) to ensure proper support. For older homes, we may install a fan-rated brace between joists to handle the weight and torque of the fan.

    Do I need to hire an electrician for ceiling fan installation? ▾
    While handy-homeowners can install fans, improper wiring, insecure mounting, or incorrect boxing can create safety hazards. Licensed electricians ensure code-compliant, safe, and long-lasting installation.

    What's the difference between indoor and outdoor ceiling fans? ▾
    Outdoor fans have moisture-resistant motors, UV-stabilized blades, and are rated for wet locations. Indoor fans are not protected against humidity and can fail quickly when used outside.

    How long does ceiling fan installation take? ▾
    Most standard indoor fan installations take 1-2 hours. Outdoor fans or those requiring new wiring or support boxing may take 2-4 hours. We provide a time estimate during the quote.
