Torrance Electrical Panel Upgrade FAQ

When does a Torrance home need a panel upgrade?

What happens during a panel upgrade in Torrance?
We inspect the service, complete a load calculation, explain the options, coordinate the utility when needed, install labeled equipment, and arrange the permit inspection.

Yes. We can evaluate a 100-amp-to-200-amp upgrade, replacement breakers, grounding, meter equipment, and service conductors. We plan around South Bay construction, garage placement, and the mix of original and renovated Torrance homes.
