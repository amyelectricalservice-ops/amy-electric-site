Frequently Asked Questions

Recessed Lighting FAQ

How much does recessed lighting installation cost in Los Angeles? +

Recessed lighting costs $150-$300 per can installed, including fixture, wiring, and labor. A typical kitchen with 6 lights runs $900-$1,800. Larger projects with dimmer switches and smart controls cost more.

How long does recessed lighting installation take? +

A typical installation takes 4-8 hours for 6-10 lights. Larger projects with multiple rooms may take 1-2 days. We work efficiently to minimize disruption.

Do I need a permit for recessed lighting in LA? +

Generally no, recessed lighting installation doesn't require a permit unless it involves structural changes or new circuits. We'll advise during your free estimate.

Can recessed lights be installed in any ceiling? +

Most ceilings can accommodate recessed lighting. We assess ceiling joists, insulation, and clearance during our on-site estimate to ensure proper installation.

What size recessed lights should I get? +

4-inch cans work best for accent lighting, 6-inch for general room lighting, and 8-inch for large open spaces. We recommend based on your room size and lighting goals.
