Electrical Services for Santa Monica

Santa Monica's mix of historic Craftsman homes, mid-century properties, and modern condos creates unique electrical needs. AMY Electric has the experience to handle everything from vintage knob-and-tube replacement in pre-war homes to smart home electrical upgrades in new construction.

⚡

EV Charger Installation

Santa Monica is one of LA's leading EV markets. We install Level 2 chargers for Tesla, Rivian, Ford, and all other EVs — including panel upgrades for older homes that need additional capacity.

🔧

Panel Upgrades

Many Santa Monica homes still have 60A or 100A electrical panels that can't support modern demand. We upgrade to 200A or 400A service with full LADWP coordination and permitting.

🔌

Whole-Home Rewiring

Pre-1960 Santa Monica homes often have outdated wiring. We provide complete rewiring with minimal wall damage, drywall repair coordination, and full code compliance.

🏢

Commercial Electrical

Santa Monica businesses trust us for tenant improvements, LED retrofits, and commercial service upgrades. From Montana Avenue to the Pier, we handle it all.

Lighting Installation

Recessed lighting, landscape lighting, and smart lighting for Santa Monica homes. We design and install systems that match your home's architecture and your lifestyle.

Safety Inspections
