As a licensed contractor serving Santa Monica, we are registered with the City of Santa Monica and authorized to pull electrical permits through their system. Santa Monica's permitting process is known for thorough plan review — budget 1 to 2 weeks longer than LADBS for complex electrical permits. We handle every step of the Santa Monica permitting process for our customers, from initial application through final inspection, so you never have to interact with the building department directly. Standard permit fees in Santa Monica are comparable to LADBS, typically $75 to $400 depending on project scope.
