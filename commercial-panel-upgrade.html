Why Upgrade?

Signs Your Commercial Panel Needs Upgrading

Frequent Tripping

Breakers that trip regularly indicate your panel can't handle current electrical loads.

Federal Pacific or Zinsco Panel

These panels are known fire hazards. Insurance companies may deny coverage if they're present.

Insufficient Amperage

100A or 200A service can't support modern business equipment, HVAC, and EV charging.

Code Violations

Failed inspections or code violations require panel upgrades to bring your building into compliance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Panel Upgrade FAQ

How much does a commercial panel upgrade cost in Los Angeles? +

Commercial panel upgrades range from $5,000-$25,000+ depending on amperage, building size, and electrical complexity. A typical 400A upgrade for a small business runs $8,000-$15,000.

How long does a commercial panel upgrade take? +

Most commercial panel upgrades take 1-3 days. Larger projects with 800A+ service or complex distribution may take up to a week. We work after hours to minimize business disruption.

Do I need a permit for a commercial panel upgrade? +

Yes, LADBS requires a permit for all commercial electrical work. Our permit fee is included in your estimate. We handle all paperwork and inspections.

What size panel do I need for my business? +

Small retail/offices typically need 200-400A. Restaurants and medical offices need 400-800A. Industrial facilities may need 1,200A+. We assess your load requirements during the free estimate.

Can you upgrade my panel without shutting down my business? +

We schedule panel upgrades during off-hours (nights, weekends) to minimize disruption. For critical facilities, we can install temporary power during the transition.
