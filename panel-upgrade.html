Panel Upgrades

What Is a Electrical Panel Upgrade?

An electrical panel upgrade replaces your existing breaker box — the metal panel that distributes electricity throughout your home — with a modern, higher-capacity unit. Most Los Angeles homes built before 1980 were wired with 60A or 100A service panels, designed before air conditioning, EV chargers, and modern appliances existed. An overloaded panel is more than an inconvenience; it's a fire and safety hazard.

AMY Electric upgrades panels to modern 200A or 400A service. We handle the entire process: LADBS permit, LADWP service upgrade request, installation, and inspection sign-off. A typical 200A panel upgrade in Los Angeles costs $2,500–$4,500 and takes 4–8 hours of on-site work.

"According to Amy, founder of AMY Electric and Licensed C-10 electrical contractor with 15+ years of experience: 'Most homes built before 1980 in the San Fernando Valley were designed with 100A service — barely enough for lights and a few appliances. When you add an EV charger, air conditioning, or a modern kitchen, that panel is already overloaded before you flip the first switch.'"

How Do I Know If I Need a Panel Upgrade in Los Angeles?

  • Breakers trip frequently, especially when running multiple appliances
  • Lights flicker or dim when other devices are turned on
  • Panel feels warm or makes buzzing/crackling sounds
  • You have a 60A or 100A service panel
  • You're adding an EV charger, heat pump, or AC unit
  • Your panel is a recalled brand (Federal Pacific, Zinsco, Pushmatic)
  • Home inspection flagged the electrical system

Per NEC Article 408, all modern panels must have proper labeling, accessible breakers, and adequate capacity for the connected load. Homes with Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels are at elevated fire risk — these brands have documented failure rates.

Questions

Panel Upgrade FAQ

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — all electrical panel upgrades in Los Angeles require a permit from the local building department, which is typically LADBS for properties within the City of Los Angeles or the specific building and safety department for other cities like Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, or Santa Monica. The permit process includes submission of load calculations per NEC Article 220, a one-line riser diagram showing the new panel configuration, scheduling and passing both a rough-in inspection (before the panel is energized) and a final inspection (after all work is complete and circuits are verified), coordination with LADWP for meter pull and reconnection if required, and obtaining a final Certificate of Completion. We handle the entire permitting process for every panel upgrade we perform, from initial application through final inspection sign-off. An unpermitted panel upgrade can create serious issues during home sale inspections and may void your insurance coverage for any electrical claim.
How long does a 200A panel upgrade take? ▾
The physical installation of a 200A panel upgrade typically takes 4 to 8 hours from start to finish, with the actual power interruption lasting 2 to 4 hours while we disconnect the old panel, install the new panel, transfer circuits, and restore power. The total project timeline, including permitting and LADWP coordination, spans 1 to 3 days for standard upgrades that do not require service mast or weatherhead replacement. Projects requiring LADWP service coordination — such as underground service conversions, service mast replacements, or meter socket relocations — can extend to 1 to 3 weeks due to LADWP scheduling availability. We schedule the physical installation after the permit is issued and all materials are on hand, and we coordinate with you to minimize the time your home is without power. Most homeowners find that the convenience of modern 200A service is well worth the brief interruption.
How much does a 200A panel upgrade cost in Los Angeles? ▾
A standard 100A to 200A panel upgrade in Los Angeles typically ranges from $2,500 to $4,500, including the new 200A panel and main breaker, all labor for removal of the old panel and installation of the new one, permit fees through your local building department, and the required LADBS or municipal inspections. Additional costs may apply for service mast or weatherhead replacement ($200 to $600), new grounding electrodes if required by current code ($200 to $500), meter socket replacement if the existing one does not match the new panel ($300 to $700), and trenching for underground service conversion ($800 to $2,500). We provide free, detailed estimates after an on-site evaluation of your existing equipment, with a full line-item breakdown of all costs, so you know the exact price before any work begins. Most 200A panel upgrades add significant value to your home and enable future EV charging and electrification upgrades.
Will my power be off during the upgrade? ▾
Yes — your power will be off during the installation, typically for 4 to 6 hours while we disconnect the old panel, install the new panel and main breaker, transfer each circuit to the new panel, and test all connections before restoring power. We schedule the installation to start early in the morning so the power interruption is during daytime hours when it is least disruptive. In most cases, we restore power the same day before we leave, so you are never without electricity overnight. If the project requires LADWP meter coordination — such as a service upgrade that needs a meter pull and reconnection — the outage window is coordinated with LADWP and is typically 4 to 8 hours. We keep you informed of the timeline throughout the day so you know exactly when to expect power restoration. For medical equipment requiring continuous power, we can discuss temporary power options before the installation.
Can I add an EV charger at the same time? ▾
Yes — adding a Level 2 EV charger during a panel upgrade is the most efficient approach because your electrician is already replacing the panel, running new circuits, and coordinating the LADWP service disconnection. When we add an EV charger circuit during a panel upgrade, the incremental cost is typically $500 to $1,200 for the EV charger circuit, compared to $800 to $1,800 if installed separately later. We provide a combined estimate for both the panel upgrade and EV charger installation so you can see the total cost and decide whether to do both at once. The EV charger circuit is installed as part of the panel upgrade work, so there is no additional power interruption beyond the normal panel upgrade outage. We also handle the EV charger permitting as part of the same permit application through LADBS or your local building department.
What brands of panels do you install? ▾
We install Square D QO, Eaton BR, and Siemens panels — all widely regarded as top-tier residential electrical panels with excellent reliability records, wide availability of breakers and accessories, and strong manufacturer warranties. Square D QO panels feature the QO snap-in breaker design with a red trip indicator and are the most popular residential panel in the United States. Eaton BR panels offer a broad range of breaker compatibility and a compact footprint. Siemens panels are known for their rugged construction and are commonly specified for commercial and residential projects throughout Los Angeles. We do not install Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic panels as replacements of the same brand, as these panels have well-documented safety issues and reliability concerns. If your home currently has one of these older panels, we recommend replacement with one of our preferred brands during your upgrade.
