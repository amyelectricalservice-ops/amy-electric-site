02 · Fan-rated box

Fan-Rated Box and Support Requirements

  • Fan-rated box required. A ceiling fan must hang from a box rated for the fan's weight and motion. A standard light box is not sufficient.
  • Weight rating. Fan-rated boxes are rated for 35–70 lbs depending on the fan.
  • Brace support. Heavy fans may need a brace bar between joists for secure mounting.
  • Existing box check. AMY Electric verifies the box rating during the site visit and upgrades if needed.

04 · Permits

Permit Considerations for Ceiling Fan Installation in LA

  • No permit for same-box swap. Replacing a fan in an existing box is typically permit-exempt.
  • Permit for new wiring. Running new cable or adding a circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on new wiring work.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does ceiling fan installation cost in Los Angeles?
Ceiling fan installation in Los Angeles costs $150 to $400 with an existing fan-rated box and wiring, or $300 to $700 with new wiring and a fan-rated box. Costs depend on ceiling height, box type, and smart/remote options. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
Do I need a fan-rated box for a ceiling fan?
Yes. A ceiling fan must hang from a box rated for the fan's weight and motion. A standard light box is not sufficient and can fail. AMY Electric installs fan-rated boxes when needed.
Can I add a ceiling fan where there is no existing box?
Yes. AMY Electric runs new wiring from a nearby circuit, installs a fan-rated box, and mounts the fan. This costs more ($300–$700) but is common in homes without existing ceiling fixtures.
Do smart ceiling fans require special wiring?
Wi-Fi or remote-control fans usually work with standard wiring plus a receiver in the canopy. Some require a neutral wire at the switch. AMY Electric confirms wiring compatibility during the site visit.
Do I need a permit for ceiling fan installation?
Replacing a fan in an existing box is usually permit-exempt. Installing new wiring or a new circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit. AMY Electric files any required permits and attends the inspection.
