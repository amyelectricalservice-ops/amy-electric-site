Why LA Homeowners Are Going Smart

Smart home technology has moved from luxury to mainstream. In Los Angeles, where energy costs are high and home values are substantial, smart electrical upgrades offer both convenience and long-term savings. Many upgrades also qualify for utility rebates and tax incentives.

Control lighting, climate, and security from anywhere. Voice control via Alexa, Google, and Siri.

Top Upgrades

6 Smart Electrical Upgrades for LA Homes

🔌

Smart Switches & Dimmers

Replace standard switches with smart switches for app and voice control. Popular brands: Lutron Caseta, Leviton Decora Smart, Wemo. Cost: $25–$60 per switch installed.

⚡

Smart Electrical Panels

Modern smart panels (Span, Leviton) provide circuit-level monitoring, control, and energy insights. Cost: $3,000–$5,000 installed.

🚗

EV Charger Smart Features

Smart EV chargers allow scheduling, solar integration, and load balancing. Cost: $1,500–$3,000 installed for Level 2.

🌡️

Smart Thermostats

Ecobee, Nest, and Honeywell smart thermostats learn your schedule and reduce energy costs. Cost: $200–$500 installed.

💡

Smart Lighting Systems

Whole-home smart lighting (Philips Hue, Lutron, LIFX) with app and voice control. Cost: $500–$3,000+ depending on scope.

🔋

Battery Storage Integration

Tesla Powerwall, Enphase, and other batteries integrate with smart panels for backup power and solar storage. Cost: $10,000–$20,000+.

Planning

Planning Your Smart Home Electrical Upgrade

1
Assess Your Current System

Older homes may need panel upgrades before adding smart technology. An electrical inspection identifies capacity and compatibility issues.

2
Choose an Ecosystem

Pick a primary platform (Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, or SmartThings) for consistent control. Avoid mixing incompatible systems.

3
Plan Neutral Wire Requirements

Most smart switches require neutral wires. Older homes without neutrals need either rewiring or specific smart switches designed for no-neutral setups.

4
Consider Wi-Fi Coverage

Smart home devices require reliable Wi-Fi. Consider mesh networks like Eero or Orbi for whole-home coverage.

5
Start with Infrastructure

If doing major upgrades, install conduit, neutral wires, and Cat6 cabling during rewiring or remodeling. Future-proofs the home.

6
Hire a Licensed Electrician

Smart home electrical work should be done by a licensed electrician. DIY electrical work is unsafe, often violates code, and can void homeowner's insurance.
