Signs You Need an Upgrade

Signs It's Time to Upgrade Your Panel

🧊

Frequently Tripping Breakers

If breakers trip regularly when multiple appliances run simultaneously, your panel likely lacks capacity.

💡

Flickering Lights

Lights dimming when AC, dryer, or oven turns on indicates your panel is near its load limit.

🔌

Two-Prong Outlets

Two-prong (ungrounded) outlets throughout the home often signal outdated wiring needing replacement.

📅

Panel Over 40 Years Old

Federal Pacific, Zinsco, and other older panels manufactured before 1990 are known safety hazards.

🚗

Planning an EV Charger

Most EV chargers require a 50A dedicated circuit. If your panel lacks space or capacity, an upgrade is needed.

🏠

Adding Major Appliances

New AC units, heat pumps, tankless water heaters, or kitchen renovations often require a panel upgrade.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Panel Upgrade Costs

How much does a 200 amp panel upgrade cost in Los Angeles? ▾
Most Los Angeles homeowners spend between $2,500 and $4,500 for a 100A to 200A panel upgrade, which includes the permit, labor, materials, and inspection. The exact cost depends on your home's wiring condition, the complexity of the panel swap, and whether additional work like a meter upgrade or LADWP coordination is needed.
Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes. All electrical panel upgrades in Los Angeles require a permit from LADBS (Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety). A city inspector must verify the work meets the California Electrical Code before the project is considered complete. AMY Electric handles all permit paperwork and inspection coordination for you.
How long does a panel upgrade take from start to finish? ▾
The physical panel replacement itself takes 4 to 8 hours for a standard upgrade. However, the full process including permit application, LADWP coordination for the power shutoff, installation, and final city inspection typically takes 1 to 3 weeks from start to finish.
