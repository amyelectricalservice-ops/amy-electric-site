Cost Factors

What Drives the Cost of a Dedicated Circuit in Los Angeles

  • Distance from the panel. A circuit 20 feet from the panel is cheap; 80 feet through finished walls is not.
  • Panel capacity. If the panel is full, a subpanel or upgrade is added to the scope.
  • Wire gauge. EV chargers and ranges need heavier copper (6 AWG or larger), which costs more per foot.
  • Surface vs. concealed. Running cable in conduit on the surface is faster than fishing through finished walls.
  • Permit and inspection. LADBS fees are included in our all-in quotes.

A site visit confirms the exact scope before any work is quoted.

03 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations in LA

  • Permit required. Adding a dedicated circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet California Electrical Code and NEC requirements.
  • Load calculation. AMY Electric performs a load calculation to confirm panel capacity.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit on new circuit work.

04 · Process

Our Dedicated Circuit Installation Process

  1. Site visit and load calculation. We assess panel capacity and appliance requirements, then provide a written estimate.
  2. Permit filing. We file the LADBS permit before work begins.

05 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does dedicated circuit installation cost in Los Angeles?
Dedicated circuit installation in Los Angeles costs $250 to $600 per circuit. Costs depend on the appliance, distance from the panel, and whether panel capacity is available. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
What appliances need a dedicated circuit?
Refrigerators, microwaves, dishwashers, washing machines, dryers, EV chargers, HVAC units, and large appliances each require a dedicated circuit per NEC. Sharing a circuit with other loads is not code-compliant.
Do I need a permit for a dedicated circuit?
Yes. Adding a dedicated circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in all-in quotes.
Can I add a dedicated circuit to a full panel?
If the panel is full, a subpanel or panel upgrade may be needed. AMY Electric performs a load calculation and recommends the right solution during the site visit.
How long does dedicated circuit installation take?
A single dedicated circuit takes 2 to 4 hours. Multiple circuits or a subpanel take a full day. LADBS inspection follows completion.
How many dedicated circuits can I add at once?
Multiple circuits are typically added in a single visit, and the cost per circuit drops when combined. A full day covers 3 to 6 circuits, including any subpanel work needed to make room.
Will a dedicated circuit fix my tripping breaker?
Only if the trip was caused by an overloaded shared circuit. If the breaker itself is failing or the load exceeds capacity, adding a circuit alone will not solve it, and the panel may need attention.
