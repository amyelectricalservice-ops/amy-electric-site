02 · Wired vs battery

Wired vs Battery Video Doorbell

  • Wired. Continuous power, better video quality, no battery maintenance. Requires existing doorbell wiring or new wiring.
  • Battery. Easier to install, no wiring needed, but requires periodic recharging and may have reduced video quality.
  • Recommendation. Wired is preferred for reliability, especially if existing doorbell wiring is present.
03 · Transformer and chime

Transformer, Chime, and Wiring Considerations

  • Transformer. Most wired video doorbells need a 16-24V transformer. Older doorbell transformers (8-10V) may need upgrading.
  • Chime compatibility. Some doorbells work with existing mechanical chimes; others need a digital chime or the brand's chime.
  • Wiring. Existing doorbell wire (usually 18-2) is often sufficient; long runs may need new cable.
  • Wi-Fi. Strong Wi-Fi signal at the door is required for reliable video streaming.
04 · Permits

Permit Considerations for Video Doorbell Installation in LA

  • No permit for same-circuit. Replacing a doorbell in an existing circuit is typically permit-exempt.
  • Permit for new wiring. Running new cable or adding a circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on new wiring work.
