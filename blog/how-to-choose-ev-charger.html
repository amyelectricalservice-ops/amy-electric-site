How to Choose the Right EV Charger for Your LA Home

With dozens of Level 2 chargers on the market, picking the right one comes down to three things: your panel's available amperage, how fast you want to charge, and whether you want smart features. Here's how Los Angeles homeowners should think about it.

⚡

Match Amperage to Your Panel

Most homes install a 40-amp or 48-amp Level 2 charger on a dedicated 240V circuit. If your panel is already near capacity, a 200-amp upgrade may be needed first. We size the circuit to your panel, not the other way around.

🔌

Hardwired vs. Plug-In

Hardwired chargers are cleaner and code-preferred for outdoor installs. NEMA 14-50 plug-in units are portable but generate more heat at the receptacle. For a permanent LA garage install, hardwired is the safer choice.

🤖

Smart Features Worth It

Wi-Fi scheduling lets you charge during LADWP off-peak hours (cheaper). Look for energy reporting, load sharing between two cars, and OCPP compatibility if you want future-proofing.

💰

Rebate Compatibility

The LADWP rebate and federal 30C tax credit apply to most ENERGY STAR chargers. We provide the paperwork and model numbers so your rebate goes through cleanly.

Questions

Choosing an EV Charger FAQ

What amp EV charger should I get? ▾
For most LA homes, a 40- or 48-amp Level 2 charger on a dedicated 240V circuit is ideal, adding 25-37 miles of range per hour. The right size depends on your panel's available capacity - we verify this before recommending a unit.
Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger? ▾
Not always. If your panel has a free 240V breaker space and sufficient amp capacity, we install a dedicated circuit without a panel change. Roughly 1 in 3 older LA homes needs a 200-amp upgrade first.
Is a smart charger worth it? ▾
Yes if you want to charge on LADWP off-peak rates and track energy use. Scheduling alone can save $100+ per year for a typical commuter.
Can I get a rebate on my charger? ▾
LADWP offers up to $500 for residential Level 2 installs, and the federal 30C credit covers 30% of equipment and install (up to $1,000). We supply the documentation.
How much does EV charger installation cost in LA? ▾
$600-$1,800 for a straightforward install near the panel; $2,000-$4,500 if a panel upgrade or long wire run is required. See our cost guide for details.
