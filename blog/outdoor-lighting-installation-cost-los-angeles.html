Cost Factors

What Drives the Cost of Outdoor Lighting in Los Angeles

  • Trenching. In-ground cable runs add labor and material cost per foot.
  • Transformer sizing. Larger systems need bigger, more expensive transformers.
  • Smart controls. Astronomical timers, zoning, and app control add hundreds.
  • Fixture grade. Coastal properties need marine-grade fixtures against salt air.
  • Permit and inspection. Line-voltage work requires LADBS permits, included in all-in quotes.

A site visit confirms the exact scope before any work is quoted.

03 · Transformer and smart controls

Transformer, Wiring, and Smart Controls

  • Transformer. Low-voltage (12V) systems need a transformer sized to the total fixture load, installed in a weatherproof enclosure.
  • Wiring. Direct-burial cable routed through the yard, often under mulch or hardscape.
  • Smart controls. App, voice, and schedule control via smart transformer or switch. Photocell and timer options available.
  • LED. All fixtures are LED for energy efficiency and long life.
Smart Controls

Smart Controls and Automation for Outdoor Lighting

  • Astronomical timers. Turn lights on at sunset and off at sunrise automatically.
  • Motion sensors. Security lighting activates only when needed, saving energy.
  • Zoning. Control landscape, security, and patio lighting independently.
  • App and voice control. Compatible with Alexa, Google, and Lutron systems.
  • Energy monitoring. Track usage and optimize schedules for lower bills.

Smart controls add $200 to $800 to a project but reduce energy use and maintenance.

04 · Permits

Permit Considerations for Outdoor Lighting in LA

  • No permit for low-voltage. 12V landscape lighting is typically permit-exempt.
  • Permit for line voltage. 120V fixtures or new circuits require an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on line-voltage work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does outdoor lighting installation cost in Los Angeles?
Outdoor and landscape lighting in Los Angeles costs $150 to $400 per fixture, or $1,500 to $5,000 for a full yard. Costs depend on fixture count, transformer size, wiring, and smart controls. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
What type of outdoor lighting is best?
LED path lights, accent/uplighting, and hardscape lighting are the most popular. LED is energy-efficient and long-lasting. AMY Electric designs a lighting plan that highlights architecture and landscape while minimizing glare.
Do I need a transformer for outdoor lighting?
Low-voltage (12V) landscape lighting requires a transformer to step down from 120V. Line-voltage (120V) fixtures do not. AMY Electric sizes and installs the transformer in a weatherproof location.
Can outdoor lighting be smart-controlled?
Yes. Smart transformers and switches allow app, voice, and schedule control. AMY Electric installs smart controls compatible with Alexa, Google, and Lutron systems.
Do I need a permit for outdoor lighting?
Low-voltage landscape lighting is usually permit-exempt. Line-voltage or new circuit work requires an LADBS electrical permit. AMY Electric files any required permits and attends the inspection.
How many outdoor lights can I add in a single project?
Most projects run 8 to 20 fixtures; larger systems are phased. A site visit determines transformer and circuit sizing before any work is quoted.
How long does outdoor lighting installation take?
A typical project takes 1 to 3 days including trenching, wiring, and testing. LADBS inspection follows if a permit is required.
Can outdoor lighting be controlled remotely?
Yes. Smart transformers and switches allow app, voice, and schedule control. AMY Electric installs smart controls compatible with Alexa, Google, and Lutron systems.
What is the best outdoor lighting for security?
