03 · Permits

Permit Considerations for Electrical Repair in LA

  • Minor repairs permit-exempt. Replacing a device, switch, or fixture does not require a permit.
  • Permit for significant work. Panel, feeder, or circuit work requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on panel or feeder work.

05 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does electrical repair cost in Los Angeles?
Electrical repair in Los Angeles costs $150 to $1,500 depending on the symptom. Minor repairs (outlet, switch) run $150–$300; moderate repairs (breaker, wiring) run $300–$800; major repairs (panel, feeder) run $800–$1,500+. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a diagnostic visit.
Why is my outlet dead?
A dead outlet can be caused by a tripped GFCI upstream, a broken device, or an open wire. AMY Electric traces the cause and repairs it, often in the same visit.
Why does my breaker keep tripping?
A tripping breaker usually indicates an overloaded circuit, short circuit, ground fault, or failing breaker. AMY Electric performs a load test and inspection to find the root cause.
Is a burning smell an emergency?
Yes. A burning smell from an outlet, switch, or panel indicates overheating or arcing — a fire hazard. Turn off the affected circuit and call a licensed electrician the same day.
Do I need a permit for electrical repair?
Minor repairs (device replacement) are usually permit-exempt. Panel, feeder, or circuit work requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection. AMY Electric files any required permits and attends the inspection.
