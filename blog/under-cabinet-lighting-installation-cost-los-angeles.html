Cost Factors

What Drives the Cost of Under-Cabinet Lighting in Los Angeles

  • Light type. LED tape is cheapest; hardwired line-voltage and puck lights cost more per foot.
  • Transformer and dimmer. Quality dimmers and drivers add cost but improve longevity.
  • Wiring. Fishing cable to a switch or panel adds labor.
  • Smart controls. App and voice control add equipment and programming time.
  • Permit and inspection. New circuit work requires an LADBS permit, included in all-in quotes.

A site visit confirms the exact scope before any work is quoted.

02 · LED tape vs puck vs hardwired

LED Tape vs Puck vs Hardwired: Which Is Best?

📏

LED Tape

Flexible strip, even light, ideal for continuous runs under cabinets. Low profile, modern look.

🔵

Puck Lights

Focused spot lights. Good for highlighting specific areas. More visible fixtures.

⚡

Hardwired (Line Voltage)

Most permanent and reliable. No transformer needed. Higher install cost.

🔋

Plug-In

Easiest and cheapest but visible cords and less clean. Not recommended for remodels.

03 · Transformer, dimmer, smart

Wiring, Transformer, Dimmer, and Smart Controls

  • Transformer/driver. Low-voltage LED tape and puck lights need a transformer to step down from 120V. Sized to the total load.
  • Dimmer. Most LED systems support dimming with a compatible dimmer switch. Standard dimmers may flicker; use LED-rated dimmers.
  • Smart controls. App and voice dimming via Lutron, Caseta, or smart switches. Requires compatible hardware.
  • Hidden wiring. AMY Electric routes wiring inside cabinets and walls for a clean look.
04 · Permits

Permit Considerations for Under-Cabinet Lighting in LA

  • No permit for same-circuit swap. Replacing fixtures on an existing circuit is typically permit-exempt.
  • Permit for new wiring. Running new cable or adding a circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on new wiring work.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does under-cabinet lighting installation cost in Los Angeles?
Under-cabinet lighting installation in Los Angeles costs $200 to $600 for a standard kitchen, or $800 to $2,000 for a full kitchen with transformer and smart controls. Costs depend on lighting type, wiring, and dimmer. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
LED tape vs puck vs hardwired — which is best?
LED tape is flexible and even, ideal for continuous runs. Puck lights give focused spots. Hardwired (line-voltage) is most permanent and reliable. AMY Electric installs all three and can advise based on your kitchen layout.
Do I need a transformer for under-cabinet lighting?
Low-voltage LED tape and puck lights need a transformer (driver) to step down from 120V. Hardwired line-voltage fixtures do not. AMY Electric sizes and installs the transformer in a hidden location.
Can under-cabinet lighting be dimmed?
Yes. Most LED under-cabinet systems support dimming with a compatible dimmer switch. Smart controls add app and voice dimming. AMY Electric installs dimmers and smart switches.
Do I need a permit for under-cabinet lighting?
Replacing fixtures in an existing circuit is usually permit-exempt. New wiring or a new circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit. AMY Electric files any required permits and attends the inspection.
How long does under-cabinet lighting installation take?
2 to 4 hours for a typical kitchen; longer for large kitchens or hardwired systems. LADBS inspection follows if a permit is required.
Can under-cabinet lighting be added to an existing circuit?
Often yes, if the circuit has spare capacity. AMY Electric checks the load before adding fixtures to avoid overloading the circuit.
