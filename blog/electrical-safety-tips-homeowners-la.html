Electrical Safety: What Every LA Homeowner Should Know

Electrical fires account for over 50,000 home fires annually in the US. Most are preventable.

Safety Tips

10 Essential Electrical Safety Tips

  1. Don't Overload Outlets
    Never plug space heaters or AC units into power strips.
  2. Test Your GFCIs Monthly
    Press TEST on GFCI outlets in bathrooms, kitchens, outdoors.
  3. Install AFCI Breakers
    Detect dangerous arcing that standard breakers miss.
  4. Know Your Panel Location
    Every adult should know how to shut off the main breaker.
  5. Extension Cords Are Temporary
    Never run under rugs or through walls.
  6. Watch for Warning Signs
    Flickering lights, warm outlets, buzzing sounds need attention.
  7. Keep Panel Accessible
    30 inches of clear working space required.
  8. Install Whole-Home Surge Protection
    Protects against grid switching and PSPS surges. Learn about our surge protection service →
  9. Use Outdoor-Rated Equipment
    Indoor equipment outdoors creates shock risks.
  10. Get Annual Inspections
    Older homes should have a professional inspection annually. Schedule a safety inspection →
Fire Prevention

Electrical Fire Prevention Checklist

  • ✅ All outlets have faceplates
  • ✅ No extension cords under rugs
  • ✅ Breakers labeled and accessible
  • ✅ GFCI outlets in all wet locations
  • ✅ Smoke detectors on every level
  • ✅ Panel is not warm or damaged

Questions

Electrical Safety FAQ

What are the most common electrical hazards in LA homes? ▾
The most common hazards are overloaded circuits from too many devices on one outlet, outdated wiring (especially aluminum or knob-and-tube), extension cords used as permanent wiring, missing GFCI protection in wet locations, and damaged or warm electrical panels. These account for the majority of preventable residential electrical fires.
How often should I have my electrical system inspected? ▾
Every 3–5 years for modern homes, and annually for homes built before 1980 or those with known issues. Older LA homes with original wiring should be inspected annually. Get an inspection if you notice flickering lights, warm outlets or switch plates, buzzing sounds, or frequent breaker trips.
Do I need whole-home surge protection in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes. The 2026 California Electrical Code now requires surge protection on all new and replacement panels. Even before that, LA's grid experiences voltage spikes from utility switching, PSPS events, and lightning. A whole-home surge protector costs $250–$600 installed and protects all your electronics and appliances.
