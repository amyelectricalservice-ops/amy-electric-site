02 · LED retrofit vs new

LED Retrofit vs New Installation

  • LED retrofit. Reuses existing fixtures with LED lamps or drivers. Lower cost ($2–$4/sq ft), faster, less waste.
  • New installation. Replaces fixtures entirely. Higher cost ($4–$6/sq ft) but allows layout changes and modern designs.
  • When to retrofit: existing fixtures are in good condition and layout works.
  • When to replace: fixtures are damaged, layout is poor, or you want smart controls.
03 · Rebates and Title 24

Rebates, Incentives, and Title 24 Compliance

  • LADWP/SCE rebates. Commercial LED upgrades qualify for rebates, often $0.10–$0.50 per kWh saved. AMY Electric helps with the application.
  • Title 24. California's energy code requires lighting power density limits and controls (occupancy sensors, daylighting, dimming) for commercial projects.
  • Controls and sensors. Occupancy sensors, daylight harvesting, and dimming reduce energy use and qualify for additional incentives.
  • Payback. Most LED retrofits pay back in 1–3 years through energy savings and rebates.
04 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations for Commercial Lighting in LA

  • Permit required. New circuits, panel work, or significant changes require an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet California Electrical Code and Title 24 requirements.
  • Safety. High-bay work requires proper lift equipment and fall protection.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does warehouse lighting installation cost in Los Angeles?
Warehouse and commercial lighting in Los Angeles costs $2 to $6 per square foot for an LED high-bay retrofit, with total projects running $5,000 to $50,000 or more. Costs depend on ceiling height, fixture count, wiring condition, and controls. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
Is an LED retrofit cheaper than new installation?
Yes. Retrofitting existing fixtures with LED lamps or drivers costs less than removing and replacing the entire fixture. A full retrofit typically runs $2–$4 per sq ft; new high-bay installation runs $4–$6 per sq ft. AMY Electric assesses which path saves more during the site visit.
Are there rebates for commercial LED lighting in LA?
Yes. LADWP and SCE offer rebates for qualifying commercial LED upgrades, often $0.10–$0.50 per kWh saved. AMY Electric helps with the rebate application and documentation. Incentives change, so we confirm current programs during the quote.
What is Title 24 and does it apply to my project?
Title 24 is California's energy code. Commercial lighting projects must meet lighting power density limits and include controls (occupancy sensors, daylighting, dimming). AMY Electric designs to Title 24 and provides compliance documentation.
How long does a warehouse lighting retrofit take?
A typical warehouse retrofit takes 2 to 7 days depending on size and ceiling height. Work is often phased to avoid disrupting operations. LADBS inspection follows completion if a permit is required.
Do I need a permit for commercial lighting in LA?
Yes for new circuits, panel work, or significant changes. Simple lamp swaps may be permit-exempt. AMY Electric files LADBS permits and attends the inspection. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in all-in quotes.
