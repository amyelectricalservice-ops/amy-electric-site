Is Your Electrical Panel Telling You Something?

Your electrical panel is the heart of your home's electrical system. Ignoring signs of age or overload can lead to fire hazards.

8 Signs

8 Signs You Need a Panel Upgrade

  • Frequent Breaker Trips
    If breakers trip regularly, your panel lacks capacity.
  • Flickering or Dimming Lights
    Lights dimming when AC or dryer turns on = near-max capacity.
  • Panel Over 40 Years Old
    Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels are known fire hazards.
  • Warm or Discolored Panel
    Warm panel, scorch marks, or burning smell needs immediate attention.
  • Using Extension Cords Permanently
    You don't have enough outlets — panel may not support needed circuits.
  • Planning an EV or Major Appliance
    EV chargers and heat pumps often need 200A service.
  • Two-Prong Outlets Throughout
    Signals outdated wiring. Panel upgrade is typically part of rewiring.
  • You Have a Fuse Box
    Fuse boxes are unsafe, inconvenient, and insufficient.
