How Much Does Whole Home Rewiring Cost?

If your home has old wiring (knob-and-tube, aluminum, or cloth-insulated), rewiring is a safety necessity.

Cost Breakdown

Rewiring Cost Breakdown

ItemTypical Range
Per Square Foot (standard)$8 – $15 per sq ft

Rewiring Process

What to Expect During Rewiring

1
Initial Assessment

Inspect current wiring and determine scope.

2
Permit & Plan Check

LADBS or your city requires permits.

3
Rough-In Wiring

Run new Romex through walls and crawl spaces.

4
Device Installation

New outlets, switches with AFCI/GFCI protection.

5
Panel Connection

Connect new circuits, disconnect old.

6
Inspection & Close-Out

City inspection and documentation.

Old Wiring Dangers

Signs Your Home Needs Rewiring

  • Knob-and-tube — Pre-1940s. No ground wire, fire hazard.
  • Aluminum wiring — 1965–1973. Prone to connection failures.
  • Cloth-insulated — Insulation crumbles when touched.
  • Two-prong outlets — No grounding, unsafe for electronics.
