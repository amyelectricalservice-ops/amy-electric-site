Old Wiring Dangers

Signs Your Home Needs Rewiring

  • Knob-and-tube — Pre-1940s. No ground wire, fire hazard.
  • Aluminum wiring — 1965–1973. Prone to connection failures.
  • Cloth-insulated — Insulation crumbles when touched.
  • Two-prong outlets — No grounding, unsafe for electronics.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Rewiring Costs

How much does whole home rewiring cost in Los Angeles? ▾
Whole home rewiring in Los Angeles typically costs $8 to $15 per square foot. For a standard 2,000 square foot home, expect to pay between $12,000 and $20,000. Larger homes of 3,000 square feet or more can cost $18,000 to $35,000 or higher. Costs include materials, labor, permits, and inspection, but wall repair and painting are typically additional.
How long does whole home rewiring take? ▾
A standard 2-bedroom rewire typically takes 1 to 2 weeks, while larger homes with 3 or more bedrooms can take 2 to 4 weeks. The timeline depends on home size, wall accessibility, number of circuits, and whether walls need to be opened. The full process from permit to final inspection can span 3 to 6 weeks including planning and closeout.
Does rewiring increase home value? ▾
Yes. Whole home rewiring significantly increases home value by eliminating fire risk from outdated wiring, supporting modern electrical demands like EV charging and smart home systems, and making the home more attractive to buyers and insurance companies. Updated electrical is one of the top improvements LA buyers look for in older homes.
