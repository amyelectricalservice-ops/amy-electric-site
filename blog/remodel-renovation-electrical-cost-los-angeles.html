Cost Factors

What Drives Remodel Electrical Costs in Los Angeles

  • Scope. A kitchen remodel costs more than a single-room refresh.
  • Wall access. Open walls during renovation make wiring cheap; finished walls raise costs.
  • Panel capacity. Adding loads may require a panel upgrade.
  • Permits. LADBS permits are required for new circuits and panel work.
  • Timeline. Coordinating with other trades affects scheduling.

A site visit confirms the exact scope before any work is quoted.

03 · Permits and timeline

Permits, Inspections, and Timeline (LADBS)

  • Permit required. Remodel electrical requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Timeline. Kitchen/bath: 1–3 days. Addition: 3–7 days. Whole-home: 1–2 weeks.
  • Inspection. LADBS inspects; we attend and provide the passed inspection record.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting, with online filing through PermitLA and ePlanLA. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit.

04 · Panel upgrade triggers

When a Panel Upgrade Is Required for a Remodel

  • Full panel. No spare breaker slots for new circuits.
  • Outdated panel. Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic panels must be replaced.
  • Load increase. New appliances, EV charger, or HVAC exceed capacity.
  • Insurance. Insurers may require a panel upgrade before issuing a policy.

07 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does remodel electrical cost in Los Angeles?
Remodel electrical in Los Angeles costs $3,000 to $15,000 or more depending on scope. Kitchen remodels run $3,000–$8,000, bath remodels $1,500–$4,000, and additions $5,000–$15,000. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
When is a panel upgrade required for a remodel?
A panel upgrade is often required when adding new circuits, large appliances, or an EV charger, or when the existing panel is full or outdated (FPE, Zinsco, Pushmatic). AMY Electric performs a load calculation during the site visit.
Do I need a permit for remodel electrical in LA?
Yes. Remodel electrical work requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in all-in quotes.
How long does remodel electrical take?
A kitchen or bath remodel electrical scope takes 1 to 3 days. A whole-home remodel or addition takes 3 to 7 days. LADBS inspection follows completion. AMY Electric coordinates with your contractor to phase the work.
Can I combine a panel upgrade with a remodel?
Yes, and it saves money. Upgrading the panel during a remodel avoids a separate project and a second permit. AMY Electric can bundle panel work into the remodel scope.
What is the LA Primary Renovation Program for landlords?
The LA Primary Renovation Program offers incentives for qualifying rental property upgrades. Electrical scope (panel, wiring, safety) may qualify. AMY Electric can advise on eligibility and documentation during the site visit.
How long does remodel electrical work take?
A single-room remodel takes 1 to 3 days; a whole-home remodel takes 1 to 3 weeks. LADBS inspection follows completion.
Do I need a permit for remodel electrical work?
New circuits, panel work, or wiring changes require an LADBS permit. Simple like-for-like replacements are usually permit-exempt. AMY Electric files any required permits and attends the inspection.
