03 · Panel upgrade first

When You Need a Panel Upgrade First

  • 100A service. Most smart-home retrofits exceed 100A capacity. A 200A upgrade ($2,500–$4,500) is usually required.
  • Full panel. If your panel has no spare breaker slots, a subpanel or upgrade is needed.
  • Older panels. Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic panels must be replaced before any smart-home work.
  • Load calculation. AMY Electric performs a load calculation during the site visit to confirm capacity.
04 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations for Smart Home Electrical in LA

  • Permit required. Panel work, new circuits, and hardwired devices require an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet California Electrical Code and Title 24 requirements.
  • EVITP-certified. AMY Electric is EVITP-certified (#4051604) for EV-ready and smart-home wiring.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does smart home electrical installation cost in Los Angeles?
A smart home electrical retrofit in Los Angeles typically costs $1,500 to $6,000. A smart panel upgrade runs $2,000–$4,000, dedicated circuits $250–$600 each, and whole-home surge protection $300–$700. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
Do I need a panel upgrade for a smart home?
Often yes. Smart panels, EV chargers, and dedicated circuits add load. Many older LA homes have 100A service that cannot support a full smart-home retrofit. AMY Electric performs a load calculation during the site visit and recommends a 200A upgrade if needed.
What smart home upgrades require an electrician?
Smart panels, subpanels, dedicated circuits for appliances, whole-home surge protectors, hardwired smart switches, and EV-ready circuits all require a licensed electrician and often a permit. Plug-in devices like smart bulbs do not.
Do I need a permit for smart home electrical work?
Yes for panel work, new circuits, and hardwired devices. Plug-in devices do not. AMY Electric files LADBS permits and attends the inspection. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
Can smart home electrical work be combined with a remodel?
Yes, and it saves money. Running new circuits and upgrading the panel during a remodel avoids opening walls twice. AMY Electric coordinates with your contractor to phase the work.
What is a smart panel?
A smart panel (e.g., Span, Leviton) lets you monitor and control individual circuits from an app, set priorities during an outage, and integrate with solar and battery storage. It replaces or augments a standard breaker panel.
