02 · California code

California Code Requirements for Smoke & CO Detectors

  • Hardwired with battery backup. California requires hardwired smoke detectors with battery backup in most homes.
  • Interconnection. All alarms must be interconnected so when one sounds, all sound.
  • Placement. Smoke detectors in every bedroom, outside sleeping areas, and on every level. CO detectors outside sleeping areas and on every level with fuel-burning appliances.
  • Remodel trigger. New construction and remodels must comply with current code.

04 · Permits

Permit Considerations in LA

  • No permit for same-circuit swap. Replacing detectors in an existing circuit is typically permit-exempt.
  • Permit for new wiring. Running new cable or adding a circuit requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on new wiring work.
