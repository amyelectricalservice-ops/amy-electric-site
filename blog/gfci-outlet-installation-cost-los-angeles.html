02 · Where GFCI is required

Where GFCI Protection Is Required (NEC 210.8)

NEC 210.8 requires GFCI protection in the following locations. California adopts these requirements, and inspectors check them during real-estate and insurance reviews.

🍳

Kitchens

Countertop receptacles and anywhere within 6 feet of a sink.

🛁

Bathrooms

All receptacles in the bathroom, including vanity and tub areas.

🚗

Garages

All 125V, 15A and 20A receptacles in attached and detached garages.

🌳

Outdoors

All outdoor receptacles, including patios, decks, and pool areas.

🧺

Laundry & Utility

Laundry areas, utility sinks, and basement receptacles.

🚰

Near Sinks

Any receptacle within 6 feet of a sink in any room.

04 · Testing and maintenance

Testing and Maintaining GFCI Outlets

  • Test monthly. Press the TEST button; the RESET button should pop out and power should cut. Press RESET to restore.
  • If TEST doesn't trip: the device has failed and needs replacement — it is no longer protecting you.
  • If RESET won't hold: there may be a ground fault or wiring issue. Call a licensed electrician.
  • Lifespan: GFCI devices typically last 10–15 years. Replace any that fail testing.
05 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations for GFCI in LA

  • No permit for same-box replacement. Swapping an existing outlet for a GFCI in the same box is typically permit-exempt.
  • Permit for new circuits. Adding a new circuit or running new cable requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. When required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. All work meets current California Electrical Code and NEC requirements.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on new circuit work.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GFCI outlet installation cost in Los Angeles?
GFCI outlet installation in Los Angeles costs $150 to $350 per outlet including labor and device. A whole-room or bath package runs $400 to $900. Prices depend on wiring condition, box type, and whether new cable must be run. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
Where is GFCI protection required by code?
NEC 210.8 requires GFCI protection in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, outdoors, laundry areas, basements, and within 6 feet of a sink. California adopts these requirements. Older homes often lack protection in some of these locations.
What is the difference between GFCI and AFCI?
GFCI protects against ground faults (shock risk, especially near water). AFCI protects against arc faults (fire risk from damaged wiring). Many modern installations require both. A dual-function breaker provides both in one device.
Do I need a permit to install a GFCI outlet?
Replacing an existing outlet with a GFCI in the same box usually does not require a permit. Adding a new circuit or running new cable does. AMY Electric files any required LADBS permits and attends the inspection.
How do I test a GFCI outlet?
Press the TEST button monthly; the RESET button should pop out and power should cut. Press RESET to restore power. If TEST does not trip the outlet, or RESET will not hold, the device has failed and needs replacement.
