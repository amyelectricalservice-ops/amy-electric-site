02 · Where GFCI is required

Where GFCI Protection Is Required (NEC 210.8)

🍳

Kitchen

All countertop outlets within 6 feet of a sink require GFCI protection.

🛁

Bathroom

All bathroom outlets must be GFCI-protected per NEC.

🚗

Garage

All garage outlets, including those for EV chargers, require GFCI.

🌧️

Outdoor

All outdoor outlets must be GFCI-protected with weatherproof covers.

🏊

Pool/Spa

Pool and spa equipment circuits require GFCI protection.

🏠

Basement

Unfinished basement outlets require GFCI protection per code.

03 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations in LA

  • Permit required. New GFCI circuits require an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • NEC 210.8. GFCI protection is required in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, outdoors, basements, and near water.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet California Electrical Code and NEC requirements.
  • Testing. All GFCI outlets are tested under load after installation.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit on new circuit work.

05 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GFCI outlet installation cost in Los Angeles?
GFCI outlet installation in Los Angeles costs $150 to $350 per outlet. Costs depend on whether a new circuit is needed, the location, and whether the panel has capacity. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
Where are GFCI outlets required?
GFCI outlets are required in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, outdoors, basements, and near any water source per NEC 210.8. LA County and LADBS enforce these requirements.
Do I need a permit for GFCI outlet installation?
Yes, if a new circuit is being added. Simple GFCI receptacle replacement on an existing circuit may not require a permit. AMY Electric determines permit requirements during the site visit and handles all permitting.
GFCI outlet vs GFCI breaker — which is better?
GFCI outlets protect one location and are easy to reset. GFCI breakers protect the entire circuit from the panel. AMY Electric recommends based on your home's wiring and the number of outlets needing protection.
How long does GFCI outlet installation take?
A single GFCI outlet replacement takes 30 to 60 minutes. New GFCI circuits take 2 to 4 hours including wiring and testing. LADBS inspection follows if a permit is required.
