Upgrade Options

Smart Home Upgrades Worth Considering

  • Smart Switches & Dimmers
    Replace standard switches for voice/app control. $50–$150 per switch.
  • Smart Outlets
    Control lamps from your phone. $75–$200 per outlet.
  • Smart Ceiling Fans
    Fan and light from one smart switch. $200–$600 installed.
  • Energy Monitoring System
    Real-time usage by circuit. $400–$900 installed.
  • Smart Smoke & CO Alarms
    Phone notifications. 10-year battery. $1,500–$3,000 whole-home.
  • Automated Outdoor Lighting
    Schedule and control from phone. $200–$1,000+.
Panel Considerations

Electrical Panel Considerations

⚡

Neutral Wires Required

Most smart switches need a neutral at the switch box. Many older LA homes lack these.

🔋

Panel Capacity

Multiple smart devices increase load. 200A panel may be needed.

📶

WiFi vs Z-Wave vs Thread

WiFi needs coverage. Z-Wave creates mesh. Thread/Matter is the future.

🏠

Matter Protocol

Cross-platform compatibility. Future-proof your investment.
