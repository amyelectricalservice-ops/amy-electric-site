03 · Transfer switches and permits

Transfer Switches and LADBS Permits for Generators in LA

  • Transfer switch required. Any generator that powers house circuits needs a transfer switch to isolate from the utility grid. Manual switches cover essential circuits; automatic switches cover the whole home seamlessly.
  • Permit required. Standby generators need an LADBS electrical permit, a mechanical permit for fuel piping, and a city inspection. Portable plus transfer switch needs an electrical permit.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS permit and inspection fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. Installation must meet CEC requirements for grounding, bonding, and clearances.

Los Angeles uses LADBS (Department of Building and Safety) for electrical permitting, with online filing through PermitLA and ePlanLA. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit on a generator.

04 · Fuel, placement, noise

Fuel, Placement, and Noise Considerations

  • Natural gas. Preferred when a gas line is available — unlimited runtime during an outage. AMY Electric can run a new gas line if needed.
  • Propane. Works where no gas line exists, but requires tank refills during extended outages.
  • Placement. Standby units need a level concrete pad, clearances from windows and doors per code, and ventilation. Setback rules apply in LA.
  • Noise. Modern standby generators run at 60–70 dB. Placement and enclosure choices reduce noise for neighbors.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does generator installation cost in Los Angeles?
Standby generator installation in Los Angeles costs $2,500 to $7,000 (unit $1,500–$4,000 plus installation $1,000–$3,000). Portable generator plus transfer switch installation runs $500 to $1,500. Costs depend on generator size, fuel type, placement, and whether a panel upgrade is needed. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit, and the LADBS permit is included.
What size generator do I need for my home?
A load calculation determines the right size. Essentials-only (fridge, lights, outlets) needs 7,500–10,000W. Whole-home coverage needs 20,000–26,000W. AMY Electric performs a load calculation during the site visit and sizes the generator to your critical circuits, not to a guess.
Do I need a permit for a generator installation in Los Angeles?
Yes. A standby generator requires an LADBS electrical permit, a mechanical permit for fuel piping, and a city inspection. A portable generator with a transfer switch requires an electrical permit. AMY Electric files all permits and attends the inspection. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
What is a transfer switch and do I need one?
A transfer switch safely connects a generator to your home's electrical system and isolates it from the utility grid during an outage. It is required by code for any generator that powers house circuits. AMY Electric installs manual or automatic transfer switches sized to your load.
Should I choose natural gas or propane?
Natural gas is preferred when a gas line is available — it provides unlimited runtime during an outage. Propane works where no gas line exists but requires tank refills. AMY Electric can run a new gas line if needed and will advise on the best fuel for your property.
How long does generator installation take?
A standby generator installation typically takes one to three days including concrete pad, fuel line, electrical connection, and testing. Portable plus transfer switch takes half a day. LADBS inspection follows completion. From signed estimate to passed inspection, most projects finish within one to three weeks.
