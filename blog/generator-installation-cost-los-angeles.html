02 · Generator types

Standby vs. Portable Generators

🏠

Standby (Whole-Home)

Permanently installed, auto-starts on outage. Powers entire home. Requires ATS and gas line.

🔌

Portable + Transfer Switch

Lower cost. Manual connection via transfer switch. Powers selected circuits during outage.

⚡

Inverter Generator

Quiet, clean power for sensitive electronics. Ideal for smaller homes or essential circuits only.

🔋

Battery Backup (Solar)

Solar + battery storage for silent backup. No fuel, no emissions, no noise.

03 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations in LA

  • Permit required. Generator installations require an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Gas line permit. Standby generators require a gas line permit and inspection by LADBS or the gas utility.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet California Electrical Code, NEC Article 702, and Title 24 requirements.
  • Utility notification. LADWP must be notified for interconnection and net metering if solar is involved.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit on generator work.

05 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does generator installation cost in Los Angeles?
Portable generator outlets cost $500 to $1,000. Manual transfer switches cost $800 to $1,500. Automatic transfer switches cost $1,500 to $3,000. Standby generators (22kW) cost $2,500 to $7,000 installed. Costs depend on generator size, transfer switch type, and gas line requirements. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
Do I need a permit for a generator installation?
Yes. Generator installations require an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection. Standby generators also require a gas line permit. AMY Electric files all permits and attends inspections. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in all-in quotes.
What size generator do I need?
A typical LA home needs 10-22kW for whole-home backup. Smaller homes or essential circuits only need 5-10kW. AMY Electric performs a load calculation to size the right generator for your needs.
Can I run a generator during a power outage?
Yes. Standby generators auto-start within seconds of an outage. Portable generators require manual connection via a transfer switch. Never connect a generator directly to your panel without a transfer switch — it is dangerous and illegal.
How long does generator installation take?
Portable generator outlets take 2 to 4 hours. Standby generator installation takes 1 to 2 days including gas line, electrical, and inspection. LADBS inspection follows completion.
