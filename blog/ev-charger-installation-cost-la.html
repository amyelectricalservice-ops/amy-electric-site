Is It Worth It?

Is Home EV Charging Worth the Investment?

For most Los Angeles EV owners, the math is clear. At current LA electricity rates (~$0.22/kWh average), charging a typical EV at home costs about $7–12 per 100 miles. Compare that to $15–22 per 100 miles for public Level 2 charging or $25–35 for gasoline.

With the $500 LADWP rebate and 30% federal tax credit, your net installation cost can be as low as a few hundred dollars — paid back in fuel savings within the first year or two. Plus, the convenience of waking up to a full charge every morning is invaluable.

Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

How much does EV charger installation cost in Los Angeles? ▾
A NEMA 14-50 outlet installation costs $350–$550. A Tesla Wall Connector runs $450–$900. Other hardwired EVSE (ChargePoint, JuiceBox) cost $500–$1,200. If your panel needs an upgrade, add $2,500–$4,500. Permit fees run $150–$400. After the LADWP rebate ($500) and federal tax credit (30%), your net cost can be significantly lower.
Does LADWP offer EV charger rebates in 2026? ▾
Yes. LADWP offers up to $500 for qualifying Level 2 residential EV charger installations. You must use a licensed electrician, pull an electrical permit, and pass inspection. The rebate can be combined with the 30% federal tax credit (up to $1,000) through 2032, making the net installation cost very affordable.
How long does EV charger installation take? ▾
Most standard installations take 3–5 hours once permits are approved. If a panel upgrade is needed, the panel work takes 4–8 hours on a separate day. Permit processing adds 1–2 weeks to the overall timeline. Your electrician handles all permitting and scheduling with LADBS.
