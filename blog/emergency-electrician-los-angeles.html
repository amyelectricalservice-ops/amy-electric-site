Emergency Types

Electrical Emergencies We Handle

⚡

Complete Power Outage

If only your home is without power, it could be a main breaker failure, meter issue, or service conductor problem.

🔥

Sparking or Smoking Outlet

An outlet that sparks, smokes, or feels hot is a fire risk. Turn off the breaker and call immediately.

💧

Flooded Electrical System

After flooding or water damage, your electrical system needs urgent inspection.

🌩️

Storm Damage

Fallen trees and lightning can pull down service wires and damage panels.

👃

Burning Smell from Panel

Indicates overheating or arcing. Serious fire risk.

🔊

Buzzing or Crackling Sounds

Suggests loose connections or arcing faults.

What to Do

What to Do in an Electrical Emergency

  1. Safety first — Keep everyone away. Don't touch the outlet, panel, or device.
  2. Turn off the breaker — If safe, flip the affected circuit to OFF.
  3. Call a licensed electrician — Call AMY Electric at (818) 302-5614.
  4. Call 911 — If there's active fire, smoke, or someone has been electrocuted.
  5. Do NOT use water — Use a Class C fire extinguisher for electrical fires.

Questions

Emergency Electrician FAQ

What counts as an electrical emergency in Los Angeles? ▾
Call immediately for sparking or smoking outlets, burning smells from your panel or outlets, exposed or downed wires, flooding around electrical equipment, a complete power outage affecting only your home, or buzzing/crackling sounds from your electrical system. These are fire and electrocution risks that require urgent professional attention.
How much does an emergency electrician cost in LA? ▾
Emergency service calls range from $95–$195 during business hours and $195–$395 after hours and holidays. The diagnostic fee is applied toward the repair cost. Breaker replacements during emergencies run $200–$500, and temporary power restoration costs $300–$800 depending on the situation.
How fast can AMY Electric respond to an emergency? ▾
We prioritize emergencies and typically arrive within 1–3 hours in the Greater Los Angeles area. Call (818) 302-5614 for immediate assistance. If there is an active fire, call 911 first — do not attempt to handle an electrical fire with water, as this can cause electrocution.
