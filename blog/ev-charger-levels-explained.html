Level 1 vs Level 2 vs Level 3 EV Chargers

EV charging "levels" describe charging speed and the electrical connection required. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right setup for home and know what to expect at public stations.

📝

Level 1 (120V)

Uses a standard household outlet. Adds 3-5 miles of range per hour. Fine for low-mileage drivers but impractical as a primary home charger in car-heavy LA households.

⚡

Level 2 (240V)

The home standard. Adds 25-37 miles per hour on a 40-48 amp circuit. Requires a dedicated 240V line from your panel - exactly what we install.

🚀

Level 3 / DC Fast (480V+)

Public only. Adds 150-300 miles in 20-30 minutes using direct-current. Not installed at homes - requires industrial utility connections.

At a Glance

Which Level Is Right for You?

1
Daily LA commuter

Level 2 at home. Overnight charging covers the average 30-mile round trip with margin to spare.

2
Second car, short trips

Level 1 may suffice if you drive under 20 miles/day and can leave it plugged in constantly.

3
Long road trips

Rely on Level 3 public networks (Electrify America, EVgo) and keep Level 2 at home for daily use.

Questions

EV Charger Levels FAQ

Can I install a Level 3 charger at home? ▾
No. DC fast chargers require 480V+ utility service and industrial infrastructure. They are only found at public and commercial stations.
Is Level 2 much faster than Level 1? ▾
Yes - about 6 to 8 times faster. Level 2 adds 25-37 miles per hour versus 3-5 for Level 1, turning an overnight charge into a few hours.
Do all EVs use the same Level 2 plug? ▾
Nearly all non-Tesla EVs in North America use the J1772 connector for Level 2. Tesla uses its own connector but includes a J1772 adapter.
How fast is a 48-amp Level 2 charger? ▾
A 48-amp charger delivers about 11 kW, adding roughly 37 miles of range per hour - enough to fully charge most EVs overnight.
What does Level 2 installation require? ▾
A dedicated 240V circuit run from your panel to the charger location, plus a permit and inspection from the city or LADWP as applicable.
