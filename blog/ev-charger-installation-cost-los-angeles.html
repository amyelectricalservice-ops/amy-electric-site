Level 1 vs Level 2

Understanding EV Charging Levels

🔌

Level 1 Charging

Uses a standard 120V household outlet. Adds 3–5 miles of range per hour. Suitable for short commutes only. Most EV owners find it too slow for daily use.

⚡

Level 2 Charging

Uses a 240V dedicated circuit. Adds 25–30 miles of range per hour. Fully charges most EVs overnight. This is what Los Angeles EV owners need.

🏠

Home vs Public

Home Level 2 charging costs about $0.04–$0.06 per mile. Public DC fast charging costs $0.12–$0.20 per mile. Home installation pays for itself within a year.

What Affects Cost

Factors That Affect Your EV Charger Installation Cost

1
Electrical Panel Capacity

A 200A panel with open breaker slots can usually accommodate a 50A EV circuit without upgrade. Older 100A panels often need upgrading first.

2
Distance from Panel to Charger

Longer cable runs from the main panel to the garage or driveway increase material and labor costs. Short runs under 50 feet are the most affordable.

3
Garage Type

Attached garages are easiest. Detached garages may require trenching for underground conduit, adding $500–$2,000 to the project.

4
Permit Requirements

Los Angeles requires a permit for any new 240V circuit installation. AMY Electric handles all LADBS permit paperwork and inspection coordination.

5
Utility Coordination

If a panel upgrade is needed, coordination with LADWP or SoCalEd for meter service changes adds time but not significant cost if handled by your contractor.

Rebates & Incentives

EV Charger Rebates in Los Angeles

Several rebate programs can reduce your out-of-pocket cost for EV charger installation:

  • LADWP EV Charger Rebate — LADWP offers rebates for residential Level 2 charger installations. Check the current program year for amounts and eligibility.
  • Southern California Edison (SoCalEd) — SoCalEd provides rebates for Level 2 charger installations in their service territory. Amounts vary by program cycle.
  • Federal Tax Credit — The Inflation Reduction Act includes a 30% tax credit (up to $1,000) for EV charger installation costs at your primary residence.
  • Employer Reimbursement — Many employers offer EV charger installation reimbursement as a workplace benefit. Check with your HR department.

Rebate programs change frequently. We recommend checking each program's current status before starting your project.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About EV Charger Installation Costs

How much does EV charger installation cost in Los Angeles? ▾
A Level 2 EV charger installation in Los Angeles typically costs $800 to $2,500 for the charger and installation combined. A NEMA 14-50 outlet installation runs $500 to $1,200. If your electrical panel needs an upgrade to support the charger, add $2,000 to $5,000 depending on the scope of work.
What is the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 EV charging? ▾
Level 1 uses a standard 120V household outlet and adds about 3-5 miles of range per hour — enough for short commutes but too slow for daily EV use. Level 2 uses a 240V dedicated circuit and adds 25-30 miles of range per hour, fully charging most EVs overnight. Level 2 is what most Los Angeles EV owners need.
Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger? ▾
Not always. If your electrical panel has available capacity (typically 200A with open breaker slots), a Level 2 charger can often be added without a panel upgrade. Homes with 100A service or older panels may need an upgrade. A licensed electrician can perform a load calculation to determine your exact needs.
Are there rebates for EV charger installation in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes. LADWP and Southern California Edison offer rebates for Level 2 EV charger installations, though amounts vary by program and year. Federal tax credits may also apply under the Inflation Reduction Act. Check current utility programs for the latest rebate amounts and eligibility requirements.
How long does EV charger installation take? ▾
A straightforward Level 2 charger or NEMA 14-50 outlet installation takes 2 to 4 hours when the electrical panel has sufficient capacity. If a panel upgrade is also needed, the combined project takes 1 to 3 days including permit and inspection.
