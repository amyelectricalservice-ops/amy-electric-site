Cost Factors

What Drives the Cost of a Panel Upgrade in Los Angeles

  • Amperage. 100A to 200A upgrades are the most common; 400A for large homes costs more.
  • Panel location. Exterior panels are cheaper to access than interior panels behind finished walls.
  • Service entrance. Upgrading the service entrance cable and meter adds cost.
  • Insurance requirements. Some carriers require specific panel types or upgrades before binding coverage.
  • Permit and inspection. LADBS fees are included in all-in quotes.

A site visit confirms the exact scope before any work is quoted.

02 · LA County cooling mandate

LA County Cooling Mandate and Panel Upgrades

Los Angeles County requires cooling (air conditioning) in certain residential buildings, which often requires a panel upgrade to support the additional load. AMY Electric can assess whether your property is affected and upgrade the panel accordingly.

03 · Insurance

Insurance Requirements for Panel Upgrades

  • Hazardous panels. Many insurers require replacement of Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic panels before issuing or renewing a policy.
  • Undersized service. 60A or 100A service may not meet insurer requirements for modern homes.
  • Restored coverage. Replacing the panel can restore standard coverage and rates.
  • Documentation. AMY Electric provides the LADBS permit and passed inspection record insurers request.
04 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations in LA

  • Permit required. A panel upgrade requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Utility coordination. LADWP or SCE may need to upgrade the service connection for higher amperage.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet current California Electrical Code requirements.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit on a panel upgrade.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a panel upgrade cost in Los Angeles?
An electrical panel upgrade in Los Angeles costs $1,500 to $5,000 depending on amperage. A 100A to 200A upgrade runs $2,500–$4,500. A 200A to 400A upgrade runs $4,500–$8,000. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
What is the LA County cooling mandate?
Los Angeles County requires cooling (air conditioning) in certain residential buildings, which often requires a panel upgrade to support the additional load. AMY Electric can assess whether your property is affected and upgrade the panel accordingly.
Will my insurance require a panel upgrade?
Many California insurers require panel upgrades for homes with Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic panels, or for homes with undersized service. Replacing the panel can restore standard coverage and rates.
Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade?
Yes. A panel upgrade requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in all-in quotes.
How long does a panel upgrade take?
The physical swap takes 4 to 8 hours with the power off. LADBS inspection follows within 48 hours. If LADWP or SCE must upgrade the service connection, add 1 to 3 weeks. Most projects finish within 1 to 3 weeks.
Will my insurance require a panel upgrade?
Many carriers require replacement of Zinsco, Federal Pacific, or Pushmatic panels before binding or renewing coverage. AMY Electric provides the inspection record and permit documentation insurers request.
Does a panel upgrade increase home value?
A modern 200A panel removes an insurance and inspection obstacle during a sale and supports EV chargers, heat pumps, and induction cooking. It is one of the higher-ROI electrical upgrades for LA homes.
