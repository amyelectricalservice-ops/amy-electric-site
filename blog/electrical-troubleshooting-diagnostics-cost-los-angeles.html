05 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations for Electrical Repairs in LA

  • Permit required for significant work. Panel repairs, feeder work, and circuit additions require an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Minor repairs usually permit-exempt. Replacing a device, switch, or fixture does not require a permit.
  • Permit fees included. When a permit is required, LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. All work meets current California Electrical Code requirements.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping a permit on panel or feeder work.

06 · Process

Our Electrical Troubleshooting Process

  1. Diagnostic visit. We test, inspect, and trace the root cause, then provide a written quote.
  2. Repair plan. We explain the cause, the fix, and the cost before any work begins.

07 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an electrical diagnostic visit cost in Los Angeles?
An electrical diagnostic service call in Los Angeles costs $150 to $350, and is typically waived if you book the repair with AMY Electric. Most repairs run $300 to $1,500 depending on the cause. We provide a firm written quote before any repair work begins.
Why does my breaker keep tripping?
A breaker that trips repeatedly usually indicates an overloaded circuit, a short circuit, a ground fault, or a failing breaker. It can also signal an undersized panel or a wiring problem. AMY Electric traces the cause with a load test and inspection, not just a breaker swap.
Is a burning smell from an outlet dangerous?
Yes. A burning smell from an outlet, switch, or panel indicates overheating, loose connections, or arcing — all fire hazards. Turn off the affected circuit at the panel and call a licensed electrician the same day. Do not use the outlet until it is inspected.
What causes flickering lights in a house?
Flickering lights can come from a loose bulb or fixture, a failing switch or dimmer, an overloaded circuit, or — more seriously — a loose service connection or failing panel. Persistent or whole-house flickering warrants a same-day diagnostic visit.
When is electrical trouble an emergency?
Treat it as an emergency if you smell burning, see sparks or smoke, have a panel that is hot to the touch, or have lost power to a critical circuit (medical equipment, sump pump). Call AMY Electric's 24/7 line at (818) 302-5614. Otherwise, schedule a diagnostic visit within a few days.
