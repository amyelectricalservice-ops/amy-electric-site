02 · Hazards

Why Cloth-Insulated Wiring Is a Hazard

  • Brittle insulation. Rubber and cloth insulation degrades and cracks, exposing live conductors.
  • Fire risk. Exposed conductors can arc and ignite surrounding material.
  • Shock risk. Cracked insulation exposes live wires to touch.
  • Overloaded circuits. Cloth wiring was designed for far lower loads than modern homes require.
  • No ground. Cloth wiring lacks a ground wire, so GFCI/AFCI protection is limited.
03 · Insurance

Insurance and Cloth-Insulated Wiring

  • Higher premiums. Homes with cloth wiring may face higher premiums or coverage restrictions.

  • Documentation. AMY Electric provides the permit and inspection record insurers request.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does cloth-insulated wiring replacement cost in Los Angeles?
Cloth-insulated wiring replacement in Los Angeles costs $8,000 to $25,000 for a whole home. Costs depend on home size, wiring accessibility, and whether the panel also needs upgrading. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
What is cloth-insulated wiring?
Cloth-insulated wiring was common from the 1920s through the 1960s. The rubber and cloth insulation degrades over time, becoming brittle and cracking, which exposes live conductors and creates fire and shock hazards.
Is cloth-insulated wiring dangerous?
Yes, when the insulation has degraded. Brittle cloth insulation can crack and expose live wires, increasing fire and shock risk. Many insurers refuse to cover homes with active cloth-insulated wiring.
Will insurance cover a home with cloth wiring?
Many California insurers refuse to write or renew policies on homes with cloth-insulated wiring, or charge higher premiums. Replacing the wiring can restore standard coverage. AMY Electric provides the permit and inspection record insurers request.
Do I need a permit to replace cloth wiring?
Yes. A whole-home or partial rewiring requires an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Permit fees ($200–$450) are included in all-in quotes.
Can I replace cloth wiring room by room?
Yes, a partial rewire of the highest-risk circuits is possible, but full replacement is recommended because the hazard is house-wide. AMY Electric quotes both scopes so you can decide.
How long does cloth wiring replacement take?
A whole-home replacement takes 5 to 14 days depending on home size and access. LADBS inspection follows completion.
Can I get insurance after replacing cloth wiring?
Yes. A completed rewire with permit and inspection records restores standard coverage with most California insurers. AMY Electric provides the documentation insurers request.
Is cloth wiring the same as knob-and-tube?
No. Cloth-insulated wiring (1920s-1960s) has rubber and cloth insulation that degrades. Knob-and-tube (pre-1940s) uses ceramic knobs and tubes with no ground. Both are hazards but require different remediation approaches.
