Generator Types

Portable vs Standby Generators

🔌

Portable Generator

$400–$1,200. Manual setup and refueling. Best for occasional outages.

🏠

Standby Generator

$3,000–$15,000+. Automatic activation. Runs on natural gas or propane.

⚡

Manual Transfer Switch

Lower cost. You manually flip between utility and generator power.

🔄

Automatic Transfer Switch

Detects power loss and starts generator automatically.
