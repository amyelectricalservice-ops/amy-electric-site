LA permits

Permits & Utility Coordination in Los Angeles

Panel upgrades in LA involve two agencies most homeowners don't expect:

LADBS Permit

Any service upgrade or panel replacement requires a building permit from LADBS. Our team pulls the permit, schedules the inspection, and handles all paperwork — you never deal with the city directly.

LADWP Coordination

If your upgrade affects the service meter or main supply, LADWP must approve the work. We coordinate with LADWP's engineering team to ensure the new panel meets utility requirements and your service is safely disconnected/reconnected.

What to Expect

  • Permit application: 1–3 business days to process
  • Inspection: Scheduled within 2–5 business days of completion
  • LADWP approval: Required for service changes — we handle the coordination
  • Reconnection: LADWP tech visits to reconnect service after inspection passes

The Complete Guide to Electrical Panel Upgrades FAQ

How much does this project cost?
Pricing depends on equipment, distance, access, service capacity, permits, and finish work. A site-specific estimate is more useful than a single internet number.
Will I need a permit?
Many installations and service changes require permits. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and scope; your electrician should explain the process before work begins.
Can my existing electrical system handle it?
Only an inspection and load calculation can answer that reliably. Panel space alone does not prove capacity.
