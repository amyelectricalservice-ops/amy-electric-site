Cost Factors

What Drives the Cost of Commercial Code Compliance in Los Angeles

  • Scope. A single-tenant space is cheaper than a multi-tenant building.
  • Title 24. Energy code upgrades for lighting and controls add cost.
  • ADA. Accessible electrical work and signage add scope.

02 · Title 24

Title 24: California Energy Code for Commercial Spaces

  • Lighting power density. Limits on watts per square foot for commercial lighting.
  • Controls. Occupancy sensors, daylighting controls, and dimming required in most spaces.
  • HVAC efficiency. Electrical scope for HVAC controls and efficiency requirements.
  • Documentation. AMY Electric provides Title 24 compliance documentation for plan check.
03 · ADA

ADA Electrical Requirements for Commercial Spaces

  • Outlet height. Accessible outlets at 15-48 inches above finished floor.
  • Switch height. Light switches and controls at accessible heights.
  • Clearance. Required clear floor space in front of electrical controls.
  • Signage. Braille and raised-character signage for electrical rooms.
04 · Permits and code

Permit and Code Considerations in LA

  • Permit required. Code compliance work requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Permit fees included. LADBS fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes.
  • Code compliance. Work must meet California Electrical Code, Title 24, and ADA requirements.
  • Inspection. LADBS inspects; we attend and provide the passed inspection record.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit on code compliance work.

06 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does commercial electrical code compliance cost in Los Angeles?
Commercial electrical code compliance in Los Angeles costs $500 to $3,000 or more depending on scope. Costs depend on the size of the space, the number of violations, and whether panel or wiring upgrades are needed. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.
What is Title 24 and does it apply to my commercial project?
Title 24 is California's energy code. Commercial projects must meet lighting power density limits, controls (occupancy sensors, daylighting, dimming), and HVAC efficiency. AMY Electric designs to Title 24 and provides compliance documentation.
Do I need ADA-compliant electrical work?
Yes. ADA requires accessible outlets, switches, and controls at specified heights and locations. Commercial remodels and new construction must comply. AMY Electric installs to ADA standards.
Do I need a permit for code compliance work?
Yes. Code compliance work requires an LADBS electrical permit and inspection. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Permit fees are included in all-in quotes.
How long does code compliance work take?
A small compliance project takes 1 to 3 days. Larger projects with panel or wiring upgrades take 1 to 2 weeks. LADBS inspection follows completion.
What is Title 24?
Title 24 is California's energy code, which sets lighting power density, controls, and efficiency requirements for commercial spaces. Compliance is verified during permit review and inspection.
Do I need ADA-compliant electrical work?
Commercial spaces open to the public must meet ADA requirements for reach ranges, signage, and accessible controls. AMY Electric coordinates the electrical scope with the overall ADA plan.
