Electrician in Beverly Hills — FAQ

Do you serve Beverly Hills for same-day repairs? ▾
Yes — we provide 24/7 emergency dispatch for urgent electrical issues. Call (818) 302-5614 anytime, day or night. We prioritize emergencies including power outages affecting only your home, sparking or smoking panels, exposed wires, and any situation involving water near electricity. Our electricians carry diagnostic equipment and common replacement parts to handle most emergencies on the first visit. Who handles permits for electrical work in Beverly Hills? ▾
Yes — we handle all permitting and inspections as part of every installation. We manage the entire process: permit applications with LADBS (Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety), plan review submissions, coordination with LADWP for service upgrades, scheduling inspections, and obtaining the final Certificate of Completion. The permit process typically takes 3-10 business days, depending on the scope of work. Permitting ensures code compliance and protects your insurance.
