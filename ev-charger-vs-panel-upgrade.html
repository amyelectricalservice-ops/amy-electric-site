Decision Guide

When Do You Need Each Service?

You Need an EV Charger If:

  • ✓ Your panel is 200A with available breaker space
  • ✓ Your load calculation shows sufficient capacity
  • ✓ You want Level 2 charging (240V, 20–35 mi/hr)
  • ✓ Your panel has 4+ open breaker slots
  • ✓ You're not adding other high-draw appliances

You Need a Panel Upgrade If:

  • ✓ Your panel is 60A or 100A
  • ✓ Breakers trip when running multiple appliances
  • ✓ You're adding EV charger + AC/heat pump/hot tub
  • ✓ Your panel is a recalled brand (Federal Pacific, Zinsco)
  • ✓ You have 0–2 open breaker slots

Do You Need Both?

Many LA homeowners need both — especially those with older 100A panels who want to add an EV charger. The good news: combining both projects into one visit saves $500–$1,200 compared to doing them separately. You avoid a second power interruption, second permit fee, and second inspection. Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install an EV charger without a panel upgrade? ▾
Yes — if your existing 200A panel has available capacity and open breaker slots. We perform a free load calculation to determine this. Many homes built after 1990 with 200A service can add an EV charger without an upgrade. Homes with 100A panels typically need an upgrade first.
How do I know what my panel capacity is? ▾
Check the label on your breaker panel door — it shows the main breaker amperage (60A, 100A, 200A). If you're not sure, we can assess during a free site visit. The main breaker number is the maximum continuous amperage your panel can handle.
What if I need both — can I do them at the same time? ▾
Yes, and it's actually more cost-effective. Combining both projects saves $500–$1,200 compared to doing them separately. You avoid a second power interruption, second permit fee, and second inspection. We provide a single combined estimate for both services.
How long does each service take? ▾
EV charger installation typically takes 2–4 hours. Panel upgrade takes 4–8 hours. If done together, expect a full day (6–10 hours). Permit and inspection scheduling adds 1–2 weeks to either project.
Do I need a permit for both? ▾
Yes — both EV charger installation and panel upgrades require LADBS permits. We handle all permitting for both services. If done together, you only pay one permit fee. Permits protect you from liability and issues during home sale.
Which should I do first? ▾
If you need both, do them together. If you're not sure, start with a free load calculation — we'll tell you exactly what your panel can handle and whether an upgrade is needed before adding an EV charger.
