The Danger

Why Federal Pacific & Zinsco Panels Must Be Replaced

These panels were installed in millions of homes from the 1950s through the 1990s. They have well-documented failure modes that no amount of maintenance can fix:

🚨 Failed Trip Tests

Federal Pacific Stab-Lok breakers fail to trip in up to 25% of overload tests per independent testing. This means your home is not protected from electrical fires.

🔥 Welded Breakers

Zinsco breakers can weld to the bus bar during a fault, preventing them from ever tripping again. The breaker stays closed while the wire overheats.

📋 Insurance Denials

Both brands are on most insurance companies' exclusion lists. You may not be able to get coverage, or your policy may exclude fire damage.

📉 Home Sale Issues

Inspectors will flag Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels. Buyers will demand replacement before closing, often at a premium price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Hazardous Panel Replacement FAQ

Are Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels dangerous? +

Yes. Both brands have well-documented failure modes. Federal Pacific Stab-Lok breakers fail to trip in up to 25% of overload tests. Zinsco breakers can weld to the bus bar during a fault, preventing them from ever tripping. Both are considered fire hazards.

How much does it cost to replace a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel? +

Replacement typically runs $2,500-$5,000 depending on the panel size, meter base condition, and whether you need a service entrance upgrade. We provide a free on-site estimate.

Will my insurance company require replacement? +

Many insurance companies will not write new policies or will cancel existing coverage if a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel is present. Replacement resolves these requirements.

Can I just replace the breakers instead of the whole panel? +

Unfortunately, no. Federal Pacific and Zinsco breakers are proprietary and no longer manufactured to safety standards. The bus bar design itself is the problem. The only safe solution is to replace the entire panel.

How do I know if I have a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel? +

Check the panel door for the brand name. Federal Pacific panels are labeled 'Stab-Lok' (1950s-1980s). Zinsco panels were installed from the 1970s through the 1990s. We'll identify it during your free inspection.
