Our Story

How AMY Electric Started

AMY Electric was founded in 2012 with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable, high-quality electrical service to Los Angeles homeowners and businesses. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted team of licensed electricians serving 16+ communities across Greater Los Angeles.

Our founder, Amy, earned her California C-10 Electrical Contractor license (#981578) after years of hands-on experience in residential and commercial electrical work. Today, we specialize in EV charger installation, panel upgrades, electrical repairs, and commercial electrical — with a focus on safety, code compliance, and customer satisfaction.

Frequently Asked

About AMY Electric

Is AMY Electric a licensed electrical contractor? ▾
Yes — AMY Electric is a licensed California C-10 electrical contractor (#981578) and EVITP-certified (#4051604). Our license is active and in good standing with the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB). You can verify our license at cslb.ca.gov.
How long has AMY Electric been serving Los Angeles? ▾
AMY Electric has been serving Greater Los Angeles since 2012 — over 14 years of residential and commercial electrical service. We've completed 200+ EV charger installations and thousands of panel upgrades, repairs, and wiring projects across 16+ LA communities.
What areas does AMY Electric serve? ▾
We serve all of Greater Los Angeles including Los Angeles, Sherman Oaks, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Santa Monica, Hollywood, North Hollywood, Studio City, Van Nuys, Encino, Beverly Hills, West LA, Culver City, Woodland Hills, and Calabasas.
What is EVITP certification? ▾
EVITP (Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Training Program) certification means our electricians are specifically trained to install EV charging equipment safely and to code. It's the industry standard for EV charger installation — required by many AHJs (Authorities Having Jurisdiction) in California.
