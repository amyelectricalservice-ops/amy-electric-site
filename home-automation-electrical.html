Frequently Asked Questions

Home Automation FAQ

Do I need an electrician for home automation? +

Yes. Most smart home systems require both high-voltage (120V/240V) and low-voltage (Cat6, speaker wire) wiring. A licensed electrician ensures proper installation and code compliance.

What smart home systems do you install? +

We wire for Lutron, Control4, Savant, Crestron, Sonos, and DIY systems like Hubitat and Home Assistant. We handle the electrical infrastructure; your integrator handles programming.

How much does smart home wiring cost? +

Basic smart lighting pre-wire starts at $2,000-$5,000. Full home automation wiring runs $8,000-$20,000. We provide detailed estimates based on your plans.

Can I add smart home features to an existing home? +

Yes, but it's easier during renovation. We can route wiring through attics and crawl spaces with minimal drywall damage. Wireless systems are also an option.

Do you program the smart home systems? +

We handle the electrical infrastructure. System programming is typically done by a smart home integrator. We work with your integrator to ensure proper connections.
