Woodland Hills Homes and Electrical Wiring

Our Process

How We Rewire Woodland Hills Homes

01

Inspection

We use thermal imaging to identify all active knob-and-tube and cloth-wired circuits in your Woodland Hills home.

02

Permits

We pull the electrical permit with LADBS and create a circuit map for your Woodland Hills property.

03

Rewire

New Romex NM-B is routed through attic, crawl spaces, and wall cavities. Old wiring is disconnected and removed.

04

Inspection

Building inspector verifies the work. You receive a signed permit and inspection certificate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Rewiring FAQ for Woodland Hills

How much does whole-home rewiring cost in Woodland Hills? +

Rewiring costs depend on home size, accessibility, and wiring condition. A typical Woodland Hills home runs $8,000-$18,000 for a full rewire. Partial rewiring of high-risk areas (kitchen, bath, attic) starts around $3,000-$6,000. We provide free on-site estimates with a written scope of work.

Do you serve all of Woodland Hills? +

Yes, we serve all neighborhoods in Woodland Hills including Woodland Hills, Warner Center, Tarzana. We're based in nearby Winnetka and can typically schedule within 2-3 business days.

How long does rewiring take in Woodland Hills? +

A full rewire typically takes 3-7 days depending on home size and attic/crawl space accessibility. Partial rewiring of specific areas takes 1-2 days. We work efficiently to minimize disruption.

Do I need a permit for rewiring in Woodland Hills? +

Yes, Woodland Hills requires an electrical permit for any rewire work. Our permit fee is included in your estimate. We handle the entire permitting process and ensure everything passes inspection.

Will rewiring increase my home value in Woodland Hills? +

Yes. Modern wiring is a major selling point in Woodland Hills. Buyers and inspectors expect grounded, code-compliant electrical systems. Homes with updated wiring sell faster and at higher prices.
