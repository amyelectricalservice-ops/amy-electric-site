How It Works

Smart Home Electrical Installation Process

1
Consultation & Needs Assessment

We discuss your smart home goals, evaluate your existing wiring, and identify which devices need neutral wires or special considerations.

2
Neutral Wire Availability Check

Many smart switches require a neutral wire in the junction box. We check your wiring and run neutrals if needed (may require accessing attic or crawlspace).

3
Permit & Planning

Most smart device installations don't require permits. New circuits, panel work, or low-voltage wiring do — we handle all permitting.

4
Power Off & Safe Work

We shut off power at the panel, verify it's dead, and work using insulated tools and PPE.

5
Wiring & Installation

Run new wiring, install devices with proper connections, and configure for app/voice control. We label everything clearly.

6
Configuration & Walkthrough

We pair devices with your network, set up scenes and automations, and show you how to use everything before we leave.

FAQ

Smart Home Electrical FAQ

Do smart switches need a neutral wire? ▾
Many smart switches (especially those requiring constant power for Wi-Fi/Zigbee) do need a neutral wire in the junction box. Older LA homes often lack neutrals at switch locations. We can run a neutral if needed or recommend no-neutral-compatible models.

Can I install smart home devices myself? ▾
While many smart devices are marketed as DIY, improper wiring, missing neutrals, or incorrect configuration can create safety hazards, reliability issues, or fail to work as expected. Professional installation ensures safety, code compliance, and proper operation.

How do I know if my home has neutral wires at switch locations? ▾
We can check during our assessment by removing the switch cover and looking in the junction box. If no neutral is present (only black, white, ground), we'll need to run one or suggest alternatives.

Do smart home devices need to be on a dedicated circuit? ▾
Generally no — smart devices draw very little power and can share circuits with outlets, lighting, or other low-load devices. However, if you're installing many devices or integrating with high-load equipment, we may recommend a dedicated circuit during the assessment.

What happens if I lose internet or power? ▾
Most smart devices revert to basic manual operation (like a regular switch) during internet or power outages. Some have battery backup for short-term operation. We recommend choosing devices with local control options that don't rely solely on cloud connectivity.

How long does smart home electrical installation take? ▾
Most single smart switch or outlet installations take 1-2 hours. Whole-home systems or those requiring new wiring or neutral runs may take 4-8 hours depending on scope and accessibility.
