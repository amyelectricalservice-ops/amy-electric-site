FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I have no power when my neighbors do? ▾
If your neighbors have power but you don't, the issue is likely inside your home or at your service panel — not with the utility. Common causes include a tripped main breaker, a blown fuse (older homes), a faulty main breaker that needs replacement, or a problem with the utility service conductors or meter pan. Less common causes include a failed meter or a disconnect at the weatherhead. We carry diagnostic equipment to pinpoint the issue fast and get your power restored safely.
Can you replace a main breaker? ▾
We can replace a faulty main breaker, repair damaged service conductors, fix meter pan issues, and coordinate with LADWP for any utility-side problems. Most main breaker replacements can be completed same-day if the part is readily available. We stock common breaker sizes and brands. For issues involving the utility meter or service drop, we coordinate with LADWP to ensure safe disconnection and reconnection. Call (818) 302-5614 for emergency dispatch.
Do you work with LADWP for power restoration? ▾
Yes — we coordinate with LADWP for meter pulls, service disconnects, and any utility-side issues that need their involvement.
