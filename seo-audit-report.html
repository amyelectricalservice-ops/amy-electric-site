If you only do one thing

Contact five relevant local organizations this week and ask for a useful link to an AMY Electric service page.

Relevant links help local customers and search engines discover the business. The goal is a small number of genuine relationships, not mass directory submissions.

  1. Choose five organizations you already serve or can genuinely help, such as property managers, EV communities, neighborhood associations, builders, or local business groups.
  2. Send this message: “Hello, I’m with AMY Electric, a C-10 electrical contractor serving Greater Los Angeles. We have a practical guide to EV charger installation and local electrical service areas. If it is useful for your members, would you consider linking to it from your resources page? We would also be glad to share your organization with customers who need your services.”
  3. Record the organization, contact date, response, and live URL in a simple spreadsheet. Check again in 30 days for new referring domains and referral visits.
