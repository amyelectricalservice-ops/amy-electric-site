What to Do During an Electrical Emergency

  • Turn off the main breaker if it's safe to access your panel and you smell burning or see sparks.
  • Evacuate the area if you see flames, thick smoke, or hear buzzing from electrical equipment.
  • Call 911 first if there's a fire or someone has been electrocuted. Do not touch the person if they're still in contact with the electrical source.
  • Do not use water to put out an electrical fire. Use a Class C fire extinguisher if available.
  • Stay away from standing water if there's been flooding — it may be electrified.
  • Call AMY Electric at (818) 302-5614 once the situation is safe. We'll dispatch a licensed electrician as quickly as possible.

If you smell burning from your electrical panel, this is a serious emergency. Turn off the main breaker immediately if it's safe to do so, evacuate the area, and call 911 if you see flames or smoke.
