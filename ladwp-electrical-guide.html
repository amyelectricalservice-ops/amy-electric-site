LADWP Overview

Understanding LADWP Electrical Requirements

LADWP (Los Angeles Department of Water and Power) is the utility provider for Los Angeles. Many electrical projects require LADWP approval, engineering, and coordination.

⚡ Meter Upgrades

Upgrading from 100A to 200A service requires a new meter base, weatherhead, and LADWP engineering approval.

🔌 Service Connections

New construction and major renovations require new service connections from LADWP. We handle the application and coordination.

💰 Rebate Programs

LADWP offers rebates for EV chargers, solar+storage, heat pump HVAC, and energy-efficient upgrades. We help you maximize savings.

📋 Permit Coordination

We handle all LADWP paperwork, engineering requests, and scheduling. You don't deal with the utility directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

LADWP Electrical FAQ

What is LADWP and how does it affect my electrical work? +

LADWP provides electricity to LA. Any work involving the service entrance, meter, or overhead service requires LADWP approval. We handle all LADWP interactions for you.

How do I get a LADWP meter upgrade? +

We submit an engineering request, coordinate the service disconnect, install the new meter base, and schedule the reconnect. The process takes 2-4 weeks.

What LADWP rebates are available? +

LADWP offers rebates for EV chargers, solar+storage, heat pump HVAC, and energy-efficient appliances. We help you identify applicable rebates.

How long does LADWP coordination take? +

Engineering requests take 1-2 weeks. Service disconnects/reconnects are scheduled 3-5 business days out. We plan around LADWP timelines.

Do I need LADWP approval for a panel upgrade? +

If you're changing the service entrance or meter base, yes. LADDP must approve and perform the disconnect/reconnect. Panel-only replacement does not require LADWP.
