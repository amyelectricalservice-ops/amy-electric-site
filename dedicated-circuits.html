How It Works

Dedicated Circuit Installation Process

1
Assessment & Load Calculation

We evaluate your electrical panel, identify available capacity, and calculate the load for your new circuit. We recommend the correct breaker size and wire gauge.

2
Permit & Planning

We file for the electrical permit with LADBS. Most dedicated circuits require a permit for safety and code compliance.

3
Power Off & Safe Work

We shut off power at the panel, verify it's dead, and work using insulated tools and PPE.

4
Wiring & Installation

Run new wiring from panel to outlet location. Install proper receptacle, breaker, and conduit as needed. Secure all connections.

5
Testing & Walkthrough

We test the circuit for proper voltage, polarity, and grounding. Show you the new breaker and label it before we leave.

FAQ

Dedicated Circuit FAQ

Do I need a permit for a dedicated circuit in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — any new circuit added to your electrical panel requires a permit from LADBS. We handle all permitting and coordination with the inspector.

Can I install a dedicated circuit myself? ▾
While handy-homeowners can run new wiring, improper breaker sizing, wire gauge, or connections can create fire hazards and fail inspection. Professional installation ensures safety, code compliance, and reliable operation.

How do I know if my panel has capacity for a new circuit? ▾
We evaluate your panel during the estimate, checking available spaces, breaker amperage, and total load calculation. Many older LA panels (60A/100A) may need an upgrade before adding new circuits.

What's the difference between a 240V outlet and a 120V outlet? ▾
240V outlets (sometimes called 220V) use two hot wires and a ground, delivering twice the voltage of standard 120V outlets. They're required for appliances like electric dryers, ranges, and EV chargers.

How long does dedicated circuit installation take? ▾
Most dedicated circuit installations take 2-4 hours depending on accessibility and wiring length. If a panel upgrade is also needed, expect additional time for the panel work (4-8 hours).
