When to Choose Portable

Portable Generators: Best for Short Outages

Pros

  • Low upfront cost ($500 – $3,000)
  • No installation required
  • Portable — take it camping or to job sites
  • Good for powering essentials (fridge, lights, phone)
  • Widely available at hardware stores

Cons

  • Must be manually started during outage
  • Limited runtime (refuel every 8-24 hours)
  • Loud — may violate LA noise ordinances
  • Carbon monoxide risk if used indoors
  • Cannot power entire home
  • Gasoline storage is a fire hazard
When to Choose Standby

Standby Generators: Best for Whole-Home Backup

Pros

  • Automatic — starts within seconds of outage
  • Powers entire home (or critical circuits)
  • Runs on natural gas — unlimited runtime
  • Quieter than portable models
  • Increases home value
  • No manual intervention required

Cons

  • Higher upfront cost ($3,000 – $15,000 installed)
  • Requires professional installation and permits
  • Permanent installation — not portable
  • Annual maintenance recommended
  • HOA approval may be required
LA-Specific Considerations

Generator Considerations for Los Angeles

Los Angeles has unique challenges that make standby generators especially valuable:

PSPS Events

PG&E and SCE implement Public Safety Power Shutoffs during high fire danger. These can last 2-5+ days. A standby generator keeps your home powered through extended outages.

Heat Waves

Summer heat waves strain the grid. A standby generator keeps your A/C running when you need it most — critical for elderly family members and medical equipment.

Earthquake Preparedness

After a major earthquake, power restoration could take days or weeks. A standby generator with natural gas provides reliable backup when the grid is down.

Frequently Asked Questions

Generator FAQ

What is the difference between a portable and standby generator? +

A portable generator is a temporary, movable power source that runs on gasoline or propane. A standby generator is a permanent, automatic system installed outside your home that runs on natural gas or propane and activates within seconds of a power outage.

How much does a standby generator cost in Los Angeles? +

Standby generators cost $3,000-$15,000 installed, depending on size and fuel type. A typical 20kW whole-home generator runs $8,000-$12,000 installed with transfer switch.

Can I install a standby generator myself? +

No. Standby generator installation requires a licensed electrician and plumber. LADBS requires permits for both the electrical transfer switch and gas connection. Our C-10 license handles the electrical side.

How long do portable generators last? +

Portable generators typically last 10-20 years with proper maintenance. However, they require regular oil changes, fuel stabilization, and periodic test runs.

Do standby generators require maintenance? +

Yes. Standby generators need annual maintenance including oil changes, filter replacement, and test runs. We offer maintenance plans to keep your generator ready.
