FAQ

Booking FAQ

How do I book an electrician visit online? ▾
Use the scheduling calendar on this page to pick a day and time that suits you. Your request goes straight to our team — we text you to confirm the window before the visit. Prefer to talk first? Call (818) 302-5614 or send the form below and a licensed electrician will call you back, usually within a few hours.
Which areas can I book for? ▾
We take online bookings across Greater Los Angeles, including Sherman Oaks, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Studio City, North Hollywood, Hollywood, Beverly Hills, West LA, Encino, Santa Monica, Van Nuys, Woodland Hills, Calabasas, and Culver City. If you are unsure whether we cover your street, call (818) 302-5614 and we will confirm in seconds.
What happens after I book? ▾
We reply with your confirmed appointment window by text. On the day, the assigned electrician texts you when en route. After the on-site assessment you get a written estimate before any work starts — no work begins without your approval.
What if I need help right now? ▾
For an urgent electrical issue, call (818) 302-5614 rather than booking online so we can discuss current dispatch availability. If there is active fire, smoke, or a severe shock hazard, call 911 first.
