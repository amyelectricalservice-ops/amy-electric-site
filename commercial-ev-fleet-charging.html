FAQ

Commercial EV Fleet Charging Questions

What is the cost of commercial EV fleet charging installation in LA? ▾
Commercial EV fleet charging in Los Angeles ranges from $1,500 to $6,000 per charging station depending on port count, panel capacity, and trenching. Multi-port Level 2 installations for fleets of 5–20 vehicles are most cost-effective when installed simultaneously with a panel upgrade.
Do you install DC Fast Charging (DCFC) for commercial fleets? ▾
Yes. AMY Electric designs and installs DCFC infrastructure for commercial fleets and apartments in LA. DCFC requires 480V three-phase electrical service, which we design, permit, and install from service entrance to charger pedestal.
Are there commercial EV charging incentives in California? ▾
Yes. LADWP offers up to $4,000 per port for qualifying businesses. The IRS Section 30C federal tax credit covers 30% of installation cost up to $100,000 per property. The CARB Clean Vehicle Rebate Project (CVRP) provides additional fleet vehicle rebates.
