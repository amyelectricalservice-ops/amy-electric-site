Long Beach EV Charger Installation FAQ

What does EV charger installation in Long Beach include?
AMY Electric evaluates your service capacity, charging location, conductor route, grounding, disconnects, and final commissioning before installing a code-compliant Level 2 charger in Long Beach.
Can you install a charger in a detached garage in Long Beach?
Yes. We can plan conduit, trenching, or an overhead route to a detached garage, then verify voltage and charging performance. We account for coastal conditions, detached garages, and the electrical demands of Long Beach remodels.
Will my Long Beach home need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?
Not always. We perform a load calculation first; options can include a dedicated circuit, load management, subpanel, or a 200-amp upgrade when the existing service is full.
Do you handle permits for EV chargers in Long Beach?
Yes. AMY Electric prepares the electrical permit, schedules the required inspection, and documents the installation so your Long Beach charger is ready for safe daily use.
