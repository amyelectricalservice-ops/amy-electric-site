Service Area Questions

What is the full service area coverage for AMY Electric? ▾
We serve Greater Los Angeles including Winnetka and the listed primary cities in the San Fernando Valley, Hollywood Hills, the Westside, and Greater LA County. Primary coverage includes Winnetka plus 16 additional cities: Los Angeles, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Encino, North Hollywood, Hollywood, Beverly Hills, West Los Angeles, Santa Monica, Van Nuys, Woodland Hills, Calabasas, and Culver City.
Do you offer same-day service in all service areas? ▾
Sometimes. Same-day emergency dispatch may be available in our primary service areas, depending on crew availability, location, traffic, and urgency. For non-emergency work, we typically schedule within 3-7 business days. Call (818) 302-5614 to confirm.
Is there a travel charge for service outside Winnetka? ▾
We do not charge a separate travel fee for service calls within the listed primary service area. Ask when scheduling if your address is outside those cities or ZIP codes.
Do you service areas outside Greater Los Angeles? ▾
Our primary focus is Greater Los Angeles and surrounding areas within LA County. If your location is outside our typical service area but close by, call us to discuss availability. We may be able to accommodate requests on a case-by-case basis.
