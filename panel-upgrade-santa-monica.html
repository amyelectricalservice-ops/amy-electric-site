How much does panel upgrade cost in Santa Monica? ▾
A standard 100A to 200A panel upgrade in Los Angeles typically ranges from $2,500 to $4,500. This includes the new panel, main breaker, labor, permits, and final inspection. Additional costs may apply if the service mast or weatherhead needs replacement, or if trenching is required for underground service. We provide free estimates after an on-site evaluation and give you a detailed line-item quote so there are no surprises. Most panel upgrades are completed in 1-2 days.
