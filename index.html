Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you licensed and insured? ▾
Yes. AMY Electric holds a California C-10 Electrical Contractor license (#981578), which you can verify on the CSLB website at cslb.ca.gov. We carry full general liability insurance and workers' compensation insurance — we will provide our certificate of insurance before any work begins. We're also EVITP-certified (#4051604) for EV charging installation, required by LADWP for their rebate program. Every electrician on our team works under Amy's C-10 license, ensuring all work meets California Electrical Code standards.
Do you pull permits? ▾
Yes — we handle all permitting and inspections with LADBS and LADWP as part of every project. Most electrical work requires a permit per the Los Angeles Municipal Code. We submit paperwork through the LADBS e-permit system, schedule inspections on your behalf, and attend the final inspection. This creates a permanent record of compliant work, which matters when you sell your home. Unpermitted work must be disclosed during a home sale.
Do you offer same-day service? ▾
When availability allows, yes — we can dispatch a licensed electrician the same day for urgent electrical issues like power outages or sparking outlets. Call (818) 302-5614 to check for same-day dispatch. For less urgent work like EV charger installations, we typically schedule within 3-7 business days. For emergencies like exposed wires, we prioritize same-day.
How much does an EV charger installation cost in LA? ▾
Most Level 2 EV charger installations run $350-$900 for a straightforward garage install. If a panel upgrade is also needed, expect $2,500-$4,500 additional. This includes a dedicated 40A circuit breaker, wiring, conduit, a NEMA 14-50 outlet or hardwired connection, and permit. LADWP offers up to $500 in rebates for qualifying Level 2 installations.
Do you serve the entire LA area? ▾
Yes — we serve the full Greater Los Angeles area including downtown LA, all neighborhoods in the San Fernando Valley, the Westside, and more. Our primary service area covers Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Encino, Van Nuys, North Hollywood, Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, West LA, Woodland Hills, Calabasas, and Culver City. See our full service area list.
How much does an electrical panel upgrade cost in Los Angeles? ▾
Upgrading from 100A to 200A service typically costs $2,500-$4,500 depending on your home's wiring, distance from the meter, and whether LADWP requires a service mast upgrade. Learn more about panel upgrades. This includes a new main breaker panel and branch circuit breakers.
How long does a panel upgrade take to complete? ▾
Most residential panel upgrades are completed in 4-8 hours. Your power will be off for most of the day, so plan accordingly. LADBS inspection is typically scheduled within 48 hours. If LADWP needs to upgrade your service connection, that adds 1-3 weeks. We handle all coordination with LADBS and LADWP.
What size EV charger do I need for my home? ▾
Most homeowners install a 40A or 48A Level 2 EV charger. A 40A charger delivers about 30 miles of range per hour — enough to fully recharge overnight. See EV charger options. We recommend a load calculation to determine your available capacity.
Do I need a permit for electrical work in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — the City of Los Angeles requires permits for most residential electrical work including panel upgrades, EV charger installations, new circuits, and rewiring. AMY Electric handles all LADBS permitting and inspection scheduling as part of every project. The permit ensures compliance with the California Electrical Code.
What is the difference between hardwired and plug-in EV chargers? ▾
Hardwired chargers connect directly to your electrical panel and can deliver higher amperage (up to 60A). Plug-in chargers connect to a NEMA 14-50 outlet and max out at 40A. Compare both types. Both require a dedicated 240V circuit and permit.
How much does a Tesla Wall Connector installation cost? ▾
Tesla Wall Connector installation runs $500-$1,200 depending on mounting location, run distance from the panel, and whether a panel upgrade is needed. Get a free Tesla charger quote. This includes a dedicated 60A circuit breaker, wiring, conduit, mounting, and permit fees. The unit itself costs $475 from Tesla.
Should I upgrade from 100A to 200A electrical service? ▾
If you plan to add an EV charger, electric water heater, heat pump, or hot tub, upgrading from 100A to 200A is recommended. Many LA homes built before 1990 still have 100A service. Compare 100A vs 200A panels. A 200A panel handles all modern electrical needs.
Do you offer commercial electrical services in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — we provide commercial electrical services including tenant improvements, LED lighting retrofits, electrical system design, sub-metering, EV fleet charging installations, and troubleshooting for LA businesses. Explore commercial services.
How much does ceiling fan installation cost in Los Angeles? ▾
Ceiling fan installation typically costs $150-$350 per fan if wiring is already in place. If new wiring or a switch is needed, expect $250-$500. Schedule your fan installation.
Do you offer surge protection for my home? ▾
Yes — we install whole-home surge protection devices at the electrical panel that protect all appliances and electronics from power surges and lightning strikes. Learn about surge protection. A Type 2 SPD provides better protection than a power strip alone.
