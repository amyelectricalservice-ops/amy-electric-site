FAQ

Electrical Safety Inspection FAQ

Do I need an electrical safety inspection if my home seems fine? ▾
Yes — many electrical hazards are hidden and not obvious to homeowners. Older LA homes (especially pre-1970) may have outdated wiring, improper grounding, or panel issues that aren't visible without professional testing.

How often should I get an electrical safety inspection? ▾
We recommend inspections every 3-5 years for most homes, or annually for homes over 40 years old, homes with aluminum wiring, or those with known electrical issues. Pre-purchase inspections are essential when buying a home.

What does an electrical safety inspection cover? ▾
We inspect the electrical panel, wiring (where accessible), outlets, switches, lighting, grounding, GFCI/AFCI protection, and look for code violations and safety hazards. We don't open walls but check accessible areas like attics, basements, and crawlspaces.

Is an electrical safety inspection the same as a permit inspection? ▾
No — permit inspections are done by LADBS to verify specific work meets code. Our safety inspection is a comprehensive evaluation of your entire electrical system for safety and code compliance, regardless of recent work.

How long does an electrical safety inspection take? ▾
Most inspections take 1-2 hours depending on home size and accessibility. Larger homes or those with complex systems may take 2-3 hours. We provide a time estimate during the quote.
