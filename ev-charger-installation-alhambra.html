Alhambra Electrical Notes: EV Charging, Panel Capacity and Local Work

Alhambra's older homes on dense residential streets commonly run on original 100A services, so EV charger installs here start with a load calculation rather than a charger catalog. San Gabriel Valley housing of this era pairs modest panels with growing EV ownership, which makes the 200A upgrade the frequent companion project to a 40A charging circuit.
