Common Issues

Electrical Problems We Fix in Los Angeles

From a single tripping breaker to a full wiring fault — we diagnose fast and fix it right. All repairs are permitted when required and performed to NEC and LA code standards.

⚡

Breakers Tripping

Frequent tripping usually means an overloaded circuit, a faulty breaker, or a short. We find the root cause — not just reset and leave.

🔌

Dead Outlets

Dead outlets can mean a tripped GFCI, loose connection, or a wiring fault. We trace it and restore power safely.

💡

Flickering Lights

Flickering usually signals loose wiring, an overloaded circuit, or a failing connection at the panel. Not something to ignore.

🔦

Partial Power Loss

If part of your home has lost power but the breaker isn't tripped, you may have a lost leg from the utility or a failing connection.

🔥

Burning Smell / Warm Outlets

Stop using that circuit immediately and call us. Burning smells or warm outlets indicate arcing — a serious fire hazard.

🔧

Aluminum Wiring

Many older LA homes have aluminum branch wiring. We safely remediate with approved COPALUM connectors or pigtailing.

🌩️

GFCI & AFCI Outlets

Bathrooms, kitchens, garages, and outdoors require GFCI protection. We install and test to current code.

🏠

Wiring Upgrades

Old knob-and-tube or cloth-wrapped wiring? We assess, repair, or replace for safety and insurance compliance.

FAQ

Electrical Repair FAQ

Do you offer same-day electrical repair? ▾
Yes — we provide 24/7 emergency dispatch for urgent electrical issues. Call (818) 302-5614 anytime, day or night. We prioritize emergencies including power outages affecting only your home, sparking or smoking panels, exposed wires, and any situation involving water near electricity. Our electricians carry diagnostic equipment and common replacement parts to handle most emergencies on the first visit. When availability allows, we dispatch same-day for non-emergency repairs as well.
Is a permit required for electrical repairs? ▾
Yes — we handle all permitting and inspections as part of every installation. We manage the entire process: permit applications with LADBS (Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety), plan review submissions, coordination with LADWP for service upgrades, scheduling inspections, and obtaining the final Certificate of Completion. The permit process typically takes 3-10 business days, depending on the scope of work. Permitting ensures code compliance and protects your insurance.
My breaker keeps tripping. Is that dangerous? ▾
A breaker that trips repeatedly is usually caused by one of three issues: an overloaded circuit (too many devices drawing power), a short circuit (a hot wire touching a neutral or ground), or a ground fault (current leaking to ground). Less common causes include a failing breaker, damaged wiring, or moisture in outlet boxes. We diagnose the root cause using specialized testing equipment including multimeters, clamp meters, and insulation resistance testers, then fix the underlying issue.
Do you work on aluminum wiring? ▾
Yes. Many 1960s–70s LA homes have aluminum branch wiring. We use approved remediation methods (COPALUM connectors or pigtailing with anti-oxidant compound) to make it safe without a full rewire.
