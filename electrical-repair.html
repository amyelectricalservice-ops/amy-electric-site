What Electrical Problems Do You Fix in Los Angeles?

From a single tripping breaker to a full wiring fault — we diagnose fast and fix it right. All repairs are permitted when required and performed to NEC and LA code standards. The National Electrical Code (NEC) requires all electrical work to be performed by a licensed electrician per Article 600.

⚡

Breakers Tripping

Frequent tripping usually means an overloaded circuit, a faulty breaker, or a short. We find the root cause — not just reset and leave.

🔌

Dead Outlets

Dead outlets can mean a tripped GFCI, loose connection, or a wiring fault. We trace it and restore power safely.

💡

Flickering Lights

Flickering usually signals loose wiring, an overloaded circuit, or a failing connection at the panel. Not something to ignore.

🔦

Partial Power Loss

If part of your home has lost power but the breaker isn't tripped, you may have a lost leg from the utility or a failing connection.

🔥

Burning Smell / Warm Outlets

Stop using that circuit immediately and call us. Burning smells or warm outlets indicate arcing — a serious fire hazard.

🔧

Aluminum Wiring

Many older LA homes have aluminum branch wiring. We safely remediate with approved COPALUM connectors or pigtailing.

🌩️

GFCI & AFCI Outlets

Bathrooms, kitchens, garages, and outdoors require GFCI protection. We install and test to current code.

Electrical Repair FAQ

Do you offer same-day electrical repair? ▾
Yes — we provide 24/7 emergency dispatch for urgent electrical repairs throughout Los Angeles. Call (818) 302-5614 anytime, day or night, including weekends and holidays. We prioritize emergencies such as complete power outages affecting only your home, sparking or smoking electrical panels, exposed live wires, burning odors from outlets or switches, and any situation involving water near electrical equipment. Our electricians carry comprehensive diagnostic tools including multimeters, clamp meters, non-contact voltage testers, insulation resistance testers, and thermal imaging cameras on every service truck, along with commonly needed replacement parts such as breakers, outlets, switches, and wiring connectors. This allows us to diagnose the root cause and complete most repairs on the very first visit. When availability permits, we also dispatch same-day for non-emergency repairs such as dead outlets, flickering lights, and breaker replacements.
Is a permit required for electrical repairs? ▾
Most minor electrical repairs — such as replacing a single outlet, switch, or light fixture with an equivalent model using existing wiring — do not require a permit. However, any repair that involves running new wiring, installing a new circuit, replacing a breaker, modifying the electrical panel, or upgrading the service requires a permit through the local building department in Los Angeles. We evaluate every repair to determine whether a permit is needed and handle the entire permitting process if required, including scheduling the rough-in and final inspections. Permitting ensures that repair work meets the current California Electrical Code, which is especially important for older Los Angeles homes where older wiring may need to be brought up to modern safety standards as part of the repair. Permitted work also provides important documentation for insurance purposes and future home sales.
My breaker keeps tripping. Is that dangerous? ▾
A breaker that trips repeatedly is your electrical system's safety mechanism working as designed, but the underlying cause of the tripping is what requires attention. Common causes include an overloaded circuit where too many devices are drawing power on a single circuit, a short circuit where a hot wire has made contact with a neutral or ground wire, a ground fault where current is leaking to an unintended path such as water or a grounded surface, or a failing breaker that has worn out from years of thermal cycling. Occasional tripping during high-load situations may not be an immediate emergency, but a breaker that trips frequently or regularly without an obvious cause needs professional diagnosis. Our electricians use specialized testing equipment to pinpoint the exact cause — measuring current draw, testing for shorts and ground faults, checking breaker trip curves, and inspecting connections throughout the circuit. We fix the root problem, not just reset the breaker.
Do you work on aluminum wiring? ▾
Yes — we are experienced in diagnosing and safely remediating aluminum branch circuit wiring, which is common in Los Angeles homes built between 1965 and 1973. Aluminum wiring was widely used during the Vietnam War era when copper prices were extremely high, but it is now known to be a significant fire hazard because aluminum expands and contracts more than copper with temperature changes, which can cause connections to loosen over time. Additionally, aluminum oxidizes differently than copper, creating a resistive layer at connection points that can generate heat. We use approved remediation methods including COPALUM connectors (the only CPSC-recommended permanent fix), AlumiConn connectors, and proper anti-oxidant compound application. We do not recommend wire nut connections for aluminum wiring — they are not approved for this application. If your home was built during this period, we recommend an inspection of your aluminum wiring connections.
