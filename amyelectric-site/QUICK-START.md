Choose one:
- **Netlify** (easiest): Drag `amyelectric-site` folder to https://app.netlify.com/drop

- Go to your domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)

## 🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING

**"Form isn't sending emails"**
→ Add your Formspree form ID to `index.html` line 284
→ Or the mailto fallback will work (opens email client)

**"Site not showing up in Google"**
→ Submit sitemap.xml to Search Console
→ Wait 1-2 weeks for indexing

**"Not ranking in Google Local Pack"**
→ Fix citations first (see CITATION-FIXES.md)
→ Get more Google reviews
→ Add more city pages

**"Images not showing"**
→ Add images to `/img/` folder (see img/IMAGE-GUIDE.txt)
→ Or site works fine without — just less visual
