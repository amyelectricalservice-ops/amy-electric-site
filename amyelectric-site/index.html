Frequently Asked Questions

Are you licensed and insured? ▾
Yes. AMY Electric holds a California C-10 Electrical Contractor license (#981578), is fully insured and bonded, and is EVITP-certified (#4051604) for EV charger installation.
Do you pull permits? ▾
Yes — we handle all permitting and inspections with LADBS, LADWP, and other local jurisdictions. You don't have to do a thing.
Do you offer same-day service? ▾
When availability allows, yes. Call (818) 302-5614 to check for same-day dispatch.
How much does an EV charger installation cost in LA? ▾
Most Level 2 EV charger installations run $350–$900 for a straightforward garage install. If a panel upgrade is also needed, expect $2,500–$4,500 additional. We provide free estimates before any work begins.
Do you serve the entire LA area? ▾
Yes — from downtown Los Angeles to the San Fernando Valley. We regularly serve Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Encino, Van Nuys, and North Hollywood. See our full service area list.
