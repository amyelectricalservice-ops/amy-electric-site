Panel Upgrades

Does Your Home Need a Panel Upgrade?

Most Los Angeles homes built before 1980 were wired with 60A or 100A service panels — designed before air conditioning, EV chargers, and modern appliances existed. An overloaded panel is more than an inconvenience; it's a fire and safety hazard.

AMY Electric upgrades panels to modern 200A service. We handle the entire process: LADBS permit, LADWP service upgrade request, installation, and inspection sign-off.

Signs You Need a Panel Upgrade

  • Breakers trip frequently, especially when running multiple appliances
  • Lights flicker or dim when other devices are turned on
  • Panel feels warm or makes buzzing/crackling sounds
  • You have a 60A or 100A service panel
  • You're adding an EV charger, heat pump, or AC unit
  • Your panel is a recalled brand (Federal Pacific, Zinsco, Pushmatic)
  • Home inspection flagged the electrical system

The Panel Upgrade Process in LA

1
Free Site Assessment

We evaluate your current service, load requirements, and meter location. We'll quote the full job with no surprises.

2
LADBS Permit Application

We file for the electrical permit with the LA Department of Building and Safety. Required for all service upgrades.

3
LADWP Coordination

We submit the service upgrade request to LADWP. They schedule the meter pull and reconnect around our installation.

4
Installation Day (4–8 Hours)

Panel replaced, circuits reconnected, new breakers installed, panel labeled. Power back on same day in most cases.

5
Inspection & Sign-Off

We schedule and attend the LADBS inspection. Once signed off, the permit is closed and your home is protected.

Questions

Panel Upgrade FAQ

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — a permit is legally required for all service upgrades in LA. AMY Electric handles all permitting with LADBS and coordinates the LADWP service upgrade and inspection. Never hire a contractor who skips the permit.
How long does a 200A panel upgrade take? ▾
The installation typically takes 4–8 hours. Factor in 1–3 weeks for LADWP service scheduling and inspection. We coordinate all of this for you.
Will my power be off during the upgrade? ▾
Yes — power is off during the installation, usually for 4–6 hours. In most cases we restore power the same day before we leave.
Can I add an EV charger at the same time? ▾
Absolutely — and we recommend it. Combining a Level 2 EV charger installation with your panel upgrade saves significant labor cost since we're already at the panel.
What brands of panels do you install? ▾
We typically install Square D QO, Eaton BR, and Siemens panels — all widely regarded as top-tier residential panels. We do not install Federal Pacific, Zinsco, or Pushmatic replacements of the same brand.
