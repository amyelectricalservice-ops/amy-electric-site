WHERE TO GET IMAGES:
-------------------

Option 1: Your Own Photos
- Take photos of recent jobs (with customer permission)
- Use your phone — just ensure good lighting
- Crop to the sizes above

Option 2: Stock Photos (Free)
- Unsplash.com — search "electrician", "electrical panel", "EV charger"
- Pexels.com — search same terms
- Download largest size available, crop to fit

Option 3: Stock Photos (Paid)
- iStock.com
- Shutterstock.com
- Search for authentic work photos, not generic stock

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT use photos with competitor logos visible
- Do NOT use photos with faces unless you have model release
- Optimize images before upload (compress to ~200-400KB using TinyPNG.com)
