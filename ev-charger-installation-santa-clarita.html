What does EV charger installation in Santa Clarita include?
AMY Electric evaluates your service capacity, charging location, conductor route, grounding, disconnects, and final commissioning before installing a code-compliant Level 2 charger in Santa Clarita.
Can you install a charger in a detached garage in Santa Clarita?
Yes. We can plan conduit, trenching, or an overhead route to a detached garage, then verify voltage and charging performance. We size work for Santa Clarita heat-pump and air-conditioning loads, longer conduit paths, and planned-community requirements.

Not always. We perform a load calculation first; options can include a dedicated circuit, load management, subpanel, or a 200-amp upgrade when the existing service is full.

Yes. AMY Electric prepares the electrical permit, schedules the required inspection, and documents the installation so your Santa Clarita charger is ready for safe daily use.
