What Commercial Electrical Services Do We Offer in Los Angeles?

"According to Amy, founder of AMY Electric and Licensed C-10 electrical contractor with 15+ years of experience: 'Commercial electrical is different from residential in almost every way — from the permit process to the code requirements to the timelines. Our team has done everything from restaurant build-outs requiring 400A three-phase service to retail tenant improvements in mixed-use buildings across the city.'"

Commercial electrical services from AMY Electric include tenant improvements, LED lighting retrofits, EV fleet charging, dedicated circuits, panel upgrades, and preventive maintenance for retail spaces, office buildings, restaurants, and light industrial facilities across Greater Los Angeles. OSHA requires commercial electrical work to comply with NFPA 70E standards for workplace electrical safety.
