Our Process

How We Rewire Santa Monica Homes

01

Inspection

We use thermal imaging to identify all active knob-and-tube and cloth-wired circuits in your Santa Monica home.

02

Permits

We pull the electrical permit and create a circuit map for your Santa Monica property.

03

Rewire

04

Inspection

Frequently Asked Questions

Rewiring FAQ for Santa Monica

How much does whole-home rewiring cost in Santa Monica? +

Rewiring costs depend on home size, accessibility, and wiring condition. A typical Santa Monica home runs $8,000-$18,000 for a full rewire. Partial rewiring of high-risk areas (kitchen, bath, attic) starts around $3,000-$6,000. We provide free on-site estimates with a written scope of work.

Do you serve all of Santa Monica? +

Yes, we serve all neighborhoods in Santa Monica including Santa Monica, West Los Angeles, Brentwood. We're based in nearby Winnetka and can typically schedule within 2-3 business days.

How long does rewiring take in Santa Monica? +

A full rewire typically takes 3-7 days depending on home size and attic/crawl space accessibility. Partial rewiring of specific areas takes 1-2 days. We work efficiently to minimize disruption.

Do I need a permit for rewiring in Santa Monica? +

Yes, Santa Monica requires an electrical permit for any rewire work. Our permit fee is included in your estimate. We handle the entire permitting process and ensure everything passes inspection.

Will rewiring increase my home value in Santa Monica? +

Yes. Modern wiring is a major selling point in Santa Monica. Buyers and inspectors expect grounded, code-compliant electrical systems. Homes with updated wiring sell faster and at higher prices.
