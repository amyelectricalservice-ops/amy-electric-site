What Is Level 2 EV Charger Installation?

Level 2 EV charger installation is the process of connecting a 240-volt charging station to your home's electrical panel via a dedicated circuit. A Level 2 charger delivers 20–35 miles of range per hour — enough to fully charge most electric vehicles overnight. This is the standard installation type for Tesla Wall Connectors, ChargePoint Home Flex, Rivian Home Charger, and all other residential Level 2 EVSE units.

Unlike a standard 120V outlet (Level 1), which adds only 4–5 miles of range per hour, Level 2 charging uses a dedicated 40–60 amp circuit on 240V power. Installation requires a licensed electrician, an electrical permit from LADBS (or your local jurisdiction), and coordination with LADWP if a service upgrade is needed.

What Rebates and Incentives Are Available for EV Charger Installation in Los Angeles?

💡 LA residents may qualify for significant EV charger rebates that can offset 50–100% of installation cost.
  • LADWP Residential EV Charger Rebate — Up to $500 for qualifying LADWP customers installing a Level 2 charger. LADWP EV Program →
  • CALeVIP — California Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Project offers rebates for charger hardware and installation at qualifying sites. CALeVIP.org →
  • Federal Tax Credit (Section 30C) — 30% of the cost of EV charging equipment (up to $1,000 for residential, more for commercial). IRS Section 30C →

As a CALeVIP-listed contractor, AMY Electric can help you identify and apply for applicable programs.

What Codes and Standards Apply?

All EV charger installations comply with the following:

  • NEC Article 625 — Electric Vehicle Power Transfer System (National Electrical Code requirement for EV charging)
  • NEC Article 210 — Branch circuits (dedicated circuit requirements for EV chargers)
  • LADBS — Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety electrical permit requirements
  • LADWP — Service upgrade coordination for 200A+ panels

References: NFPA 70 (NEC) · LADBS · LADWP

How It Works

Our EV Charger Installation Process

1. Free On-Site Evaluation

We visit your home to assess your electrical panel capacity, existing 240V availability, wall condition, and distance from the panel. This 30-minute inspection determines whether we can use existing service or need a panel upgrade. You receive a written estimate with no obligation.

2. Quote & Scheduling

You receive a detailed written estimate breaking down charger unit cost (if needed), installation labor, permitting fees, and any electrical upgrades. Typical timeline: 2-4 weeks from contract to final inspection sign-off. We work around your schedule.

3. Permit Submission

We handle all paperwork with LADBS and LADWP — permit applications, electrical plans, and coordination. Typical permit turnaround: 5-10 business days. You don't need to do anything during this step.

4. Installation (1-2 Days)

Our electricians run the 240V circuit from your breaker panel to the charger location, mount the charger, connect the circuit, install weatherproof covers, and perform all safety tests — continuity, resistance, and voltage drop verification. Every installation meets NEC Article 625 requirements.

5. Final Inspection & Sign-Off

LADBS or LADWP inspector verifies the installation. We coordinate scheduling and attend the inspection. Once approved, you receive the Certificate of Completion — required for warranty claims and future home sales.

Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

How long does EV charger installation take? ▾
Most residential Level 2 installations take 2–4 hours. If a panel upgrade is also needed, plan for a full day.
Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger? ▾
Not always. We perform a free load calculation to determine if your existing panel can support a dedicated 40A or 50A circuit. Many homes can add a charger without a panel upgrade.
Are there rebates for EV charger installation in LA? ▾
Yes. LADWP offers up to $500 for residential customers. CALeVIP provides additional incentives for qualifying installations. The federal Section 30C tax credit covers 30% of equipment cost. We can help you identify what you qualify for.
Do you install chargers in apartments or condos? ▾
Yes — in dedicated parking spots or garages where the tenant or HOA has approval. California law (AB 2565) gives tenants the right to install EV chargers in most cases. We've handled many MUD installations.
Do you handle the permit? ▾
Yes — we pull all required permits and schedule inspections. A permit is required for all new EV charger circuits in LA, and it protects you from liability and future sale complications.
