Level 2 EV Charging

Why Level 2 Is Right for Your Home

A standard 120V outlet (Level 1) adds roughly 4–5 miles of range per hour — too slow for daily driving. A Level 2 charger on a dedicated 240V circuit delivers 20–35 miles per hour, fully charging most EVs overnight.

AMY Electric installs all major Level 2 chargers and NEMA 14-50 outlets. As an EVITP-certified contractor (#4051604), we're trained specifically in EV infrastructure — from load calculations and circuit sizing to LADWP interconnection requirements.

Rebates & Incentives in Los Angeles

💡 LA residents may qualify for significant EV charger rebates that can offset 50–100% of installation cost.
  • LADWP Residential EV Charger Rebate — Up to $500 for qualifying LADWP customers installing a Level 2 charger.
  • CALeVIP — California Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Project offers rebates for charger hardware and installation at qualifying sites.
  • Federal Tax Credit (Section 30C) — 30% of the cost of EV charging equipment (up to $1,000 for residential, more for commercial). Consult your tax advisor.

As a CALeVIP-listed contractor, AMY Electric can help you identify and apply for applicable programs.

Questions

EV Charger Installation FAQ

How long does EV charger installation take? ▾
Most residential Level 2 installations take 2–4 hours. If a panel upgrade is also needed, plan for a full day.
Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger? ▾
Not always. We perform a free load calculation to determine if your existing panel can support a dedicated 40A or 50A circuit. Many homes can add a charger without a panel upgrade.
Are there rebates for EV charger installation in LA? ▾
Yes. LADWP offers up to $500 for residential customers. CALeVIP provides additional incentives for qualifying installations. The federal Section 30C tax credit covers 30% of equipment cost. We can help you identify what you qualify for.
Do you install chargers in apartments or condos? ▾
Yes — in dedicated parking spots or garages where the tenant or HOA has approval. California law (AB 2565) gives tenants the right to install EV chargers in most cases. We've handled many MUD installations.
Do you handle the permit? ▾
Yes — we pull all required permits and schedule inspections. A permit is required for all new EV charger circuits in LA, and it protects you from liability and future sale complications.
