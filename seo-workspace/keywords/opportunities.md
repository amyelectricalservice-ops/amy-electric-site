## SERP shape & competitive notes — `[directional: web]`

No SERP API is available (OpenSEO's `get_serp_results` returns HTTP 401), so these are observations
from live web fetches of the pages that currently hold these topics, **not** confirmed rankings.

- **Emergency.** Competitors lead with a phone number in the title/hero and 24/7 language
  (`911electrics.com/emergency-electrician-los-angeles-ca/` structures its whole page around
  "Get Your Free Quote" + "Emergency Electrical Services (24/7)" + FAQs). AMY's page title is
  already good (`Emergency Electrician Los Angeles 24/7 | AMY Electric`); the page body is the problem.
- **Rewiring.** `theelectricconnection.com/house-rewire/` (3,817 words) answers "How Can I Tell If I
  Need a House Rewire Without Opening the Walls?" and "What Is The Cost for A House Rewire in Los
  Angeles?" — question-shaped H2s aimed at exactly the queries AMY gets impressions for. AMY's
  `/whole-home-rewiring` is *longer* (4,285 words), so depth is not the gap.
- **Panel.** `theelectricconnection.com/panel-upgrades/` (4,100 words) is built around brand-specific
  hazards and insurance — plus separate pages for Zinsco, Federal Pacific, Pushmatic and old fuse
  boxes. AMY covers Zinsco only, in one blog post.
- **EV.** Competitors publish **per-manufacturer** EV pages (Tesla, Ford, Nissan, Porsche, Hyundai,
  Mitsubishi) and Tesla-specific install detail. AMY has strong EV assets (EVITP, estimator,
  gallery) but consolidates brand intent into fewer URLs.
