### Search Intent

**Transactional, with a strong research/comparison overlay.** Two sub-intents must not be merged:
- *Generic* (`ev charger installation los angeles`) — the searcher wants a licensed installer, usually
  after buying a car; price and rebate questions dominate.
- *Branded* (`tesla charger installation`, `tesla charger vs others`) — a Tesla owner with a Wall
  Connector already in the box, comparing hardwired 48A vs NEMA 14-50.

AMY holds the strongest genuine credential in this market (EVITP #4051604). Google rewards a
transactional service page with price ranges, rebate detail, and a booking/call action — with the
comparison material on separate pages so it doesn't dilute the money page.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities

**Topic gaps:**
- **Per-manufacturer coverage.** Competitors run a page per EV brand. AMY consolidates. A single
  generic EV page cannot win `tesla charger installation` (338 impr) *and* brand queries.
- **`tesla charger vs others`** (61 impr, **pos 17.3**) and `tesla connector alternatives`
  (68 impr, pos 31.7) are near-page-one comparison queries — the existing blog post ranks 30.4 and
  gets 589 impressions. That intent belongs on a comparison page, not the money page.
- **`property benefits ev charger`** (59 impr, pos 57.9) and multi-family/landlord EV intent — no AMY
  page owns this; `/commercial-ev-charger-installation` is the closest.
- **`ev charger installation benefits`** (84 impr, pos 97.2) — badly positioned, informational intent.

**Depth gaps:**
- Rebate content exists on the service page but AMY also has
  `/blog/ladwp-ev-charger-rebate-guide-2026`. The rebate answer should be summarised on the money page
  and linked out, not split.
- **EVITP's value is never explained.** AMY holds the certification competitors are writing explainer
  pages about but doesn't explain what it means for the customer.

**Quality gaps:**
- `/tesla-charger-installation`: 825 impressions, 0 clicks, pos 56.9. High demand, no capture.
- EV positions across the bucket are the worst on the site (many 55–97) despite the best credentials —
  an authority/link problem (see the EVITP directory gap).
