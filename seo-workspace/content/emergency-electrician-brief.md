### Search Intent

**Urgent commercial / transactional.** The searcher has no power, a burning smell, or a dead circuit
and wants someone on the phone *now*. Google rewards a landing page with a visible phone number,
24/7 language, service-area confirmation and an immediate call action — not an essay.
Audience: LA homeowners and small landlords, on mobile, in a hurry (mobile average position 26.6 vs
desktop 43.3 site-wide — `[gsc: Devices.csv]`).

### Content Gaps and Opportunities

**Topic gaps (nobody answers these):**
- What actually happens after you call — who answers, what the dispatcher asks, how a technician is
  dispatched, and the realistic window for a San Fernando Valley call.
- What to do *before* the electrician arrives — kill the main breaker, don't reset a tripping breaker
  repeatedly, when to call LADWP instead of an electrician.
- Which emergencies an electrician solves vs which are a utility problem (LADWP outage vs
  AMY service call) — genuinely useful disambiguation the competitors skip.

**Depth gaps:**
- AMY's page never states a response expectation, an after-hours process, or an emergency price
  posture. Competitors lead with 24/7 on every page.
- No indication that AMY handles the permit/utility side of a burned panel or mast — which is the
  expensive emergency.

**Quality gaps:**
- 14 of the site's 18 striking-distance queries land here and **none** of them convert
  (0 clicks from 404 impressions on the primary alone). That is a snippet + above-the-fold problem.

### Unique Angle and Information Gain

**Publish the dispatch process, not a promise.** Every LA competitor repeats "24/7" and "same-day".
None of them tell the searcher what will actually happen in the next ten minutes. AMY can publish
the real sequence (triage questions → technician assignment → estimated arrival → diagnosis quoted
before work starts), plus a genuine "this is LADWP's problem, not an electrician's" decision box, and
back it with 387 real project photos no competitor can match. That is first-hand operational
experience, not "more detail".
