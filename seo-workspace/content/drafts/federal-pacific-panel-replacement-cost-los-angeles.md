## FAQ (emit 4 required; 6 recommended — copy below maps 1:1 to FAQPage `mainEntity`)

**Q1 — How much does it cost to replace a Federal Pacific panel in Los Angeles?**
Federal Pacific (FPE) Stab-Lok panel replacement in Los Angeles runs $2,800 to $4,800 with remediation. The range includes removal of the hazardous panel, installation of a modern breaker panel, LADBS permit and inspection, and any wiring remediation needed to meet code. Homes with additional hazards (knob-and-tube, ungrounded circuits) land at the top of the range. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.

**Q2 — Why is a Federal Pacific panel dangerous?**
Federal Pacific Electric (FPE) Stab-Lok breakers have a documented history of failing to trip during overcurrent conditions, which can allow wiring to overheat and ignite. The CPSC investigated the issue, and many insurers now refuse to write or renew homeowner policies on homes with FPE panels. Replacement is the only compliant and insurable path; no breaker swap or "fix" makes an FPE panel safe.

**Q3 — Do I need a permit to replace a Federal Pacific panel?**
Yes. A panel replacement requires an LADBS electrical permit and a city inspection against the California Electrical Code. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Permit and inspection fees ($200–$450) are included in our all-in quotes. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping the permit.

**Q4 — Will my insurance cover a Federal Pacific panel replacement?**
Some insurers offer a credit or discount after documented replacement, and many require replacement before issuing or renewing a policy. AMY Electric provides the permit record and passed inspection report that insurers and escrow officers request. Check with your carrier for any available credit; we do not bill insurance directly.

**Q5 (optional) — How long does a Federal Pacific panel replacement take?**
The physical swap takes four to eight hours with the power off. LADBS inspection typically follows within 48 hours. If LADWP or SCE must upgrade the service connection, add one to three weeks of utility-side time. From signed estimate to passed inspection, most replacements finish within one to three weeks.

**Q6 (optional) — Can I sell a home with a Federal Pacific panel?**
Buyers and inspectors routinely flag FPE panels, and escrow often requires replacement or a credit before closing. Replacing the panel before listing removes the objection and produces a permit and inspection record that supports the sale price. Many sellers recover the cost in a faster, cleaner escrow.
