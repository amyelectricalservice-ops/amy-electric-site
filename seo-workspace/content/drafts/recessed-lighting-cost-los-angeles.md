## FAQ (emit 4 required; 6 recommended — copy below maps 1:1 to FAQPage `mainEntity`)

**Q1 — How much does recessed lighting installation cost in Los Angeles?**
Recessed lighting in Los Angeles typically costs $150 to $350 per can installed, including the fixture, housing, and labor. A full room package runs $900 to $3,000 depending on fixture count, ceiling type, and switching. A standard kitchen or living room with six to eight cans lands near $1,200 to $2,400. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit, and the LADBS permit is included when one is required.

**Q2 — Do I need a permit for recessed lighting in Los Angeles?**
Often yes. Adding new circuits or modifying existing wiring for recessed lighting requires an LADBS electrical permit, and the finished work must pass a city inspection against the California Electrical Code. AMY Electric files the permit, schedules the inspection, and attends it. Replacing like-for-like fixtures on an existing circuit may not require a permit, but new circuits, dimmer additions, or smart controls do.

**Q3 — How long does recessed lighting installation take?**
A typical room with six to eight cans takes four to eight hours. Larger whole-home projects run one to three days. LADBS inspection, when required, typically follows within 48 hours of completion. Accessible attic or crawlspace wiring speeds the job; finished ceilings with no attic access add time for fishing wire.

**Q4 — What is an IC-rated recessed light?**
IC-rated means the housing is safe for direct contact with insulation. Non-IC housings require a three-inch clearance from insulation and are not allowed in insulated ceilings. In Los Angeles attics, IC-rated LED housings are the standard choice and cost roughly the same as non-IC units while meeting Title 24 and fire-safety requirements.

**Q5 (optional) — Can I add dimmers or smart switches to recessed lighting?**
Yes. Dimmer switches add $100 to $250 per location including the switch and labor, and smart dimmers add $150 to $350. AMY Electric can pair recessed lighting with smart home electrical upgrades for app and voice control, and dedicated circuits for high-load zones.

**Q6 (optional) — Does recessed lighting increase home value?**
Yes. Recessed lighting is one of the highest-ROI lighting upgrades for kitchens and living rooms, and it pairs well with whole-home rewiring or panel upgrades in older LA homes. A permitted installation also produces an inspection record that supports resale and insurance documentation.
