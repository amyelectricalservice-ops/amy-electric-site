10. ACTIONABLE TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================

  HIGH PRIORITY:

  1. Title Tag Optimization
     - Current: "Electrician Los Angeles | AMY Electric"
     - Recommended pattern: "Los Angeles Electrician | [Service] | AMY Electric"
       or "Expert Electrical Contractor in Los Angeles | AMY Electric"
     - Add phone number or "Licensed C-10" for click-through rate boost.

  2. Increase Page Count
     - Branover (255 pages) and DPG (80 pages) dominate with scale.
     - Create city-specific service pages (e.g., "ev-charger-installation-
       sherman-oaks.html") to match Branover's 25+ location pages.
     - Add dedicated case studies section with 5-10 detailed project pages.

  3. Blog Growth
     - Branover has 170 blog posts; AMY has 10.
     - Target 30+ posts minimum. Focus on long-tail keywords like "how much
       does EV charger installation cost in Los Angeles" and "panel upgrade
       cost in Sherman Oaks."

  MEDIUM PRIORITY:

  4. Schema Enhancement
     - Add Place/PostalAddress with full business address to all pages.
     - Add Organization markup with sameAs (social profiles).
     - Add OpeningHoursSpecification (competitors are doing this).
     - Consider moving to automated schema generation tool.

  5. Social Proof
     - Add customer count, years in business, warranty info to homepage hero.
     - Request and display Google/Yelp reviews prominently.
     - Competitors prominently display "Since 1985", "7000+ customers", etc.

  6. Emergency/24-7 Messaging
     - Multiple competitors lead with "24/7 emergency service" in their hero.
     - AMY should prominently display emergency availability if applicable.

  7. Geo Sitemap
     - Create a geo-specific sitemap for location-based SEO.
     - Only 911 Electric has one, which is a competitive advantage.

  LOW PRIORITY:

  8. Social Media Profiles
     - Create and link Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn profiles in schema.

  9. Case Studies
     - Add 3-5 detailed project case studies with before/after photos.

  10. Qmerit or EVITP Certification Badges
     - Bryden uses Qmerit certification as a trust signal.
     - AMY already has EVITP #4051604 — display this more prominently on
       EV charger pages.
