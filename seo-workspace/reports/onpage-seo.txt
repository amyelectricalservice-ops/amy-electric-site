1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Overall impression: The site has solid SEO fundamentals — good canonical tags,
proper meta robots, strong JSON-LD structured data, and clear internal linking.
However, there are several areas where on-page SEO can be improved for better
organic search performance.

Key strengths:
  - Every page has a canonical URL, robots meta, and Open Graph / Twitter Cards.
  - JSON-LD structured data is thorough (Electrician, Service, FAQPage, BlogPosting).
  - Internal linking is robust with nav, footer, related services, and breadcrumbs.
  - All pages use descriptive, keyword-rich URLs.
  - All images have preload hints with srcset for responsive.

Key issues found:
  - 4 of 8 pages have title tags under 50 characters (ideal: 50-60).
  - 2 of 8 pages have meta descriptions under 100 characters (too short).
  - 0 of 8 pages have <img> tags with alt text in the visible HTML body (images
    are either CSS backgrounds, preloaded, or referenced only in JSON-LD/OG).
  - Blog and Testimonials pages have especially thin meta descriptions.
  - City pages have slightly generic content with repetitive sentence structures.
  - Several pages lack true h3-h4 depth in heading hierarchy.

   JSON-LD/OG tags. There is not a single <img> tag with an alt attribute on
