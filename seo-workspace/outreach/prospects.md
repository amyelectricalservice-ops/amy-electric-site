> directory and offers a single claim form; confirm any fee or eligibility terms with

directories.* AMY holds EVITP #4051604, which CALeVIP makes a hard requirement for

apartment-association audiences.

## Target criteria & guardrails

- **Topical fit**: only prospects inside Greater Los Angeles, California real estate,
  the EV/electrical trade, or utility/state incentive programs.
- **No spam outreach**: no thin PBNs, paid link farms, or irrelevant directories. Any
  paid placement (AAGLA Business Council, chamber tiers, SRAR sponsorship) must be
  bought for its *business* value and legible as paid — never disguised as editorial.
- **Anchor text safety**: natural brand anchors only — `AMY Electric`,
  `AMY Electric Los Angeles`, `licensed LA electrician`, `EVITP-certified electrician`.
- **Contact discipline**: only contacts actually fetched in this session are listed.
  Where a contact is missing, the row says so; find it before sending.
- **Claim confirmation**: every factual business claim inside an outreach draft
  (service area, incentive handling, insurance) needs a one-line confirmation from
  the owner before it is sent. Nothing in this document invents one.
- **Internal first**: the fastest wins on this site are internal, not external — geo
  service pages carry only ~3 inbound internal links each while ranking at/near
  position 1 on tiny impression counts. Fix internal linking before spending hours
  on outreach. [repo: internal-link audit of `city-*.html` / geo service pages, this session]
