Permit Fees

LADBS Electrical Permit Costs

Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety (LADBS) charges permit fees based on the scope of electrical work. Here are typical costs:

Panel Upgrade Permit

Covers main panel replacement or upgrade. Includes plan review and final inspection.

$150 - $300

Rewiring Permit

Covers whole-home or partial rewiring. Fee scales with home size.

$200 - $500

EV Charger Permit

Covers Level 2 or DC fast charger installation.

$100 - $250

New Construction Permit

Covers new construction, additions, and ADUs. Includes plan review.

$300 - $800

* Fees are approximate and may vary. We include the actual permit fee in your estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Electrical Permit FAQ

How much does an electrical permit cost in Los Angeles? +

LADBS electrical permit fees are based on the scope of work. Typical fees: panel upgrade $150-$300, rewiring $200-$500, EV charger $100-$250, new construction $300-$800. We include the permit fee in your estimate.

Who pulls the electrical permit? +

We pull all electrical permits on your behalf. As a licensed C-10 contractor, we are authorized to pull permits with LADBS. You don't need to deal with the permitting process.

How long does it take to get an electrical permit? +

Most electrical permits are issued within 1-3 business days through LADBS's online portal. Complex projects may require plan review (5-10 business days).

Do I need a permit for EV charger installation? +

Yes. Los Angeles requires an electrical permit for any EV charger installation. Our permit fee is included in your installation estimate.

What happens if I do electrical work without a permit? +

Unpermitted electrical work can result in fines, forced removal, insurance claim denials, and problems when selling your home. Always pull permits.
