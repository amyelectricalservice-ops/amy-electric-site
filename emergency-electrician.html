FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency electrical service? ▾
Yes — AMY Electric provides 24-hour emergency electrical service 7 days a week, 365 days a year throughout Greater Los Angeles, including weekends, holidays, and overnight hours. Electrical emergencies don't follow business hours, and neither do we. Our emergency electricians are on standby around the clock and can typically arrive within 60 to 90 minutes for urgent calls anywhere in our service area. When you call our emergency line at (818) 302-5614, you speak directly with a licensed electrician who can assess the situation, provide immediate safety instructions, and dispatch the nearest available crew. We handle all types of electrical emergencies including power outages, sparking or smoking panels, exposed wires, downed service lines, water damage affecting electrical systems, and any situation where there is an immediate risk of fire or electrical shock. We carry common replacement parts and diagnostic equipment on every truck to resolve most emergencies on the first visit.
How fast can you get to my location? ▾
We prioritize emergency electrical calls and dispatch the nearest available electrician to your location as quickly as possible. For emergencies within the Los Angeles city limits and most surrounding communities including Sherman Oaks, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, and the San Fernando Valley, our typical arrival time is 60 to 90 minutes from your initial call. More distant locations within our service area such as Santa Monica, Calabasas, and Beverly Hills may have slightly longer arrival times of 90 to 120 minutes depending on traffic conditions and crew availability. When you call, we provide an estimated arrival time based on the current location of our nearest available electrician and real-time traffic conditions. For life-threatening emergencies involving exposed live wires, arcing or sparking equipment, or active electrical fires, we coordinate with the Los Angeles Fire Department and can often have an electrician on site within 30 to 45 minutes.
What qualifies as an electrical emergency? ▾
An electrical emergency is any situation that poses an immediate risk of fire, electrical shock, or property damage. Common electrical emergencies that warrant an immediate call to our 24/7 line include complete or partial power outage that is affecting only your home while neighbors have power, sparking or smoking electrical panels, outlets, or switches, exposed or damaged live wires, burning smells coming from outlets, switches, or the electrical panel, water near electrical equipment such as a flooded basement or leaky pipe above the panel, a breaker that will not stay reset or trips immediately, arcing sounds from the panel or outlets, downed power lines on or near your property, and any electrical system damage from storms, earthquakes, or vehicle accidents. If you are uncertain whether your situation qualifies as an emergency, err on the side of caution and call us anyway — we can help you assess the risk over the phone. For life-threatening situations involving active fire, severe electrical shock, or downed power lines, call 911 first, then contact us.
