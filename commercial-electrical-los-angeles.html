FAQ

Commercial Electrical in Los Angeles — FAQ

What commercial electrical services do you provide? ▾
Switchgear and service upgrades, subpanels and bus duct, meter banks, tenant improvements, commercial lighting and Title 24 compliance, dedicated equipment circuits, EV fleet charging, troubleshooting, and preventive maintenance — for offices, retail, restaurants, warehouses, and multifamily properties across Los Angeles.
Can you work nights and weekends to avoid downtime? ▾
Yes — most commercial work is scheduled nights, early mornings, or weekends. Shutdowns are planned in writing with your facilities team in advance, circuits are verified dead and labeled, and we restore and test before your staff or customers arrive. Emergency dispatch runs 24/7 at (818) 302-5614.
Do commercial projects need permits in Los Angeles? ▾
Almost always. LADBS permits cover service upgrades, new panels and subpanels, tenant improvements, and most lighting overhauls, plus Title 24 energy compliance for lighting. We prepare submittals, pull permits, attend inspections, and deliver signed-off records for your files.
Are you licensed and insured for commercial work? ▾
Yes — AMY Electric holds California C-10 license #981578 in good standing with the CSLB, verifiable at cslb.ca.gov. We carry general liability and workers compensation insurance and provide certificates of insurance naming your property or management company as required.
How do you price commercial projects? ▾
After a free on-site walkthrough we deliver a written scope with line-item pricing — labor, materials, permits, and schedule — before work starts. Change orders, if site conditions demand them, are priced in writing before proceeding. No open-ended time-and-materials surprises.
Can you add EV charging for our fleet or tenants? ▾
Yes — as EVITP-certified installers (#4051604) we design commercial EV charging from single dual-port stations to multi-stall fleet depots with load management, metering, and access control. We handle utility coordination, rebates, and phased installs that grow with your fleet.
Do you offer maintenance plans for buildings? ▾
Yes. Scheduled thermal inspections of panels and switchgear, torque verification, lighting retrofits and relamping programs, and priority emergency response. Maintenance customers get documented panel schedules and first-call dispatch when something fails at 2 a.m.
