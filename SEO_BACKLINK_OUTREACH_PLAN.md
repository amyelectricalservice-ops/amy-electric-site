# AMY Electric — Month 6 Backlink Outreach Plan

**Objective:** Earn relevant, editorial mentions for AMY Electric without buying links or mass-mailing. Build a qualified prospect list of **50–100 domains**; this plan does not claim that any links have been acquired.

## Prospect framework (target 50–100 domains)

Build 8–15 prospects in each category, deduplicated by root domain:

1. Greater LA neighborhood and city news sites
2. Local chambers of commerce and business associations
3. Homeowner, neighborhood, and community organizations
4. EV clubs, local clean-transportation groups, and charging communities
5. ADU, remodeling, real-estate, and home-inspection professionals
6. Property managers, HOAs, and multifamily housing resources
7. Local nonprofits and sponsorship/community partners
8. California energy, safety, and consumer-information publishers
9. Trade schools, workforce programs, and industry associations
10. Local podcasts, newspapers, radio, and journalists

**Prospecting methods:** Search city + topic combinations, inspect “resources” pages, review competitor mentions for ideas, use public association/member directories, and identify journalists who have recently covered EVs, outages, fire prevention, or remodeling. Verify every contact path manually.

## Qualification rules

Score each prospect 0–2 for each criterion:

- Local or topical relevance
- Real editorial content and identifiable publisher
- Audience overlap with LA homeowners, businesses, EV owners, or property professionals
- Likely legitimate relationship or useful contribution
- Indexable page and clear contact/editorial policy

Prioritize scores **8–10**, nurture **5–7**, and reject **0–4**. Reject sites with spun content, hacked pages, undisclosed paid-link demands, irrelevant casino/medical/loan link farms, excessive outbound links, or no verifiable ownership. A domain must earn its place through relevance, not a metric alone.

## Ethical linkable assets

- LA electrical safety checklist and emergency decision tree
- Permit/load-calculation explainer for EV chargers and panel upgrades
- Redacted project case studies with photos and outcomes
- Local EV charging and rebate resource (verify dates before publishing)
- Expert quote from AMY’s licensed electrician
- Neighborhood-specific outage/safety guidance
- Co-hosted homeowner or property-manager safety webinar

## Outreach sequence and templates

Send one personalized email, one useful follow-up after 5–7 business days, then stop. Use a real sender, truthful subject, and opt out of further contact.

## Safeguards against spam and risk

- Never buy links, exchange links as a condition, automate scraping/email, or submit to hundreds of directories.
- Keep outreach one-to-one and explain the specific value for that publisher’s audience.
- Honor CAN-SPAM and applicable privacy rules; include identity and a simple stop-contact option.
- Do not promise rankings, payment, reciprocal links, or guaranteed publication.
- Use `rel="sponsored"`/`nofollow` when a placement is paid or otherwise required by the publisher.
- Review anchor text for natural brand/service phrasing; avoid exact-match repetition.
- Maintain a suppression list and remove contacts immediately on request.
- Check every acquired mention for accuracy, NAP, license claims, and malware before recording it as a success.
