FAQ

Lighting Installation in Los Angeles — FAQ

What types of lighting do you install? ▾
We install pendant lights, chandeliers, recessed (can) lighting, track lighting, under-cabinet and closet lighting, ceiling fans with lights, landscape lighting circuits, dimmers, and smart switches from Lutron, Leviton, and other major brands — in homes and commercial spaces across Los Angeles.
Can you hang a heavy chandelier from my ceiling? ▾
Yes, provided the box is rated for the weight. Standard nail-on boxes are not enough for heavy fixtures: we install fan-rated brace boxes that span between joists and are rated for 70+ pounds, plus proper support for chandeliers over stairwells and two-story entries. We verify structure before hanging anything heavy.
Why do my LED lights flicker or buzz with the dimmer? ▾
Flicker and buzz almost always mean the dimmer and the LED lamps are incompatible — older leading-edge dimmers chop power in ways LED drivers dislike. We replace the dimmer with an LED-rated model matched to your exact lamps and test every level from 100 percent down to minimum before we leave.
Can recessed lighting be added to plaster ceilings? ▾
Yes. We lay out the grid to avoid joists, cut clean holes with hole saws and plaster blades, and set old-work (remodel) housings that clamp to the finished ceiling — no large drywall removal. Insulation-contact (IC) rated housings go wherever insulation is present, per code.
Are you licensed for lighting work in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — AMY Electric holds California C-10 license #981578, verifiable at cslb.ca.gov, and carries general liability plus workers compensation insurance. Lighting circuits, new boxes, and dimmer/switch work are all performed to California Electrical Code and backed by our workmanship warranty.
How long does a lighting installation take? ▾
A single fixture swap takes about an hour. A kitchen pendant layout or a chandelier with new bracing typically takes half a day. Full-room recessed grids (6 to 10 cans) usually complete in one day, including dimmer setup and cleanup. Whole-home projects are scheduled in phases around your routine.
Do I need a permit to add lighting in Los Angeles? ▾
Simple fixture-for-fixture replacements generally do not. New circuits, new recessed layouts, and panel work do require LADBS permits, which we handle including scheduling the inspection. We tell you upfront, in writing, whether your project needs one — no surprises.
