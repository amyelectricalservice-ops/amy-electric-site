Burning Smell from Electrical Panel — Emergency Response

A burning smell from your electrical panel is a serious emergency that requires immediate attention. It often indicates overheating wires, a failing breaker, or melted insulation inside the panel. If you smell burning from your panel, turn off the main breaker if safe to do so and call AMY Electric immediately. We provide emergency dispatch throughout Los Angeles and the San Fernando Valley. Our electricians will diagnose the source of the smell, replace damaged components, and ensure your panel is safe.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I smell burning from my panel? ▾
If you smell burning from your electrical panel: (1) Turn off the main breaker if it's safe to access — stand to the side when switching it. (2) If the smell is strong or you see smoke, evacuate the home and call 911. (3) Call us immediately at (818) 302-5614 for emergency dispatch. Do not attempt to open the panel or investigate yourself — panels contain lethal voltages even when the main breaker is off. We have licensed electricians on call 24/7 who can safely diagnose and repair the issue.
