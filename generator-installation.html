Why Generator Installation?

Backup Power for Los Angeles Homes and Businesses

Los Angeles power outages are increasingly common — from PSPS shutoffs during wildfire season to grid strain during heat waves. A properly installed generator keeps your home or business running when the grid goes down. AMY Electric installs standby generators, portable generator transfer switches, and whole-home backup systems with permits and inspections.

Generator Types

Standby vs. Portable Generators

🏠 Standby (Whole-Home)

Permanently installed, auto-starts within seconds of an outage. Runs on natural gas or propane. Powers the whole home including AC, EV charger, and kitchen. Requires a transfer switch, concrete pad, and permits.

🔌 Portable + Transfer Switch

A portable generator connected via a manual transfer switch. You start it and flip the switch during an outage. Lower cost than standby. Good for essential circuits (fridge, lights, outlets).

⚡ Inverter Generator

Quiet, fuel-efficient portable units. Safe for sensitive electronics. Ideal for small homes, apartments, or job sites. Can be connected via transfer switch.

🔋 Battery Backup (Solar)

Tesla Powerwall or Enphase battery systems. Silent, no fuel, seamless switchover. Best paired with solar panels. Higher upfront cost, lower operating cost.

Cost Factors

What Drives the Cost of Generator Installation in Los Angeles

  • Generator size. Whole-home standby units (14-22 kW) cost more than partial-load units, but run everything including AC and EV charger.
  • Fuel source. Natural gas is cheapest to connect where gas is available; propane needs a tank and regulator.
  • Transfer switch. An automatic transfer switch is required for standby units and adds to the scope.
  • Permits. LADBS electrical and gas permits, plus seismic anchoring on a concrete pad.
  • Site work. Concrete pad, gas line trenching, and electrical runs add cost on larger lots.
  • Panel capacity. If the panel is full, a subpanel or upgrade is added to the scope.

A site visit confirms the exact scope before any work is quoted.

Installation Process

Our Generator Installation Process

  1. Site visit and load calculation. We assess your power needs, panel capacity, and fuel availability, then provide a written estimate.
  2. Permit filing. We file LADBS electrical and gas permits before work begins.
  3. Site preparation. Concrete pad, seismic anchoring, and gas line installation (if needed).

Permits & Code

Permit and Code Considerations for Generator Installation in LA

  • Electrical permit. Required for all generator installations. LADBS electrical permit and inspection.
  • Gas permit. Required for standby generators connected to natural gas or propane.
  • Seismic anchoring. Generators must be anchored to a concrete pad per California seismic requirements.
  • Clearance. Minimum clearance from windows, doors, and combustible materials per manufacturer specs and code.
  • Transfer switch. An automatic or manual transfer switch is required to prevent backfeeding the grid.

Los Angeles uses LADBS for electrical permitting. We file all permits and attend every inspection. Permit fees ($200-$450) are included in all-in quotes.

LA Considerations

Los Angeles Climate and Grid Considerations for Generators

  • PSPS events. Public Safety Power Shutoffs during wildfire season can last hours or days. A standby generator provides seamless backup.
  • Heat waves. Grid strain during extreme heat causes outages. A generator keeps AC running when it matters most.
  • Fuel availability. Natural gas is the most reliable fuel in LA. Propane tanks need refilling; diesel degrades in storage.
  • Seismic requirements. California requires seismic anchoring on a concrete pad for all standby generators.
  • Noise ordinances. LA has noise restrictions; we select quiet generators and install them with proper setbacks.
Frequently Asked Questions

Generator Installation FAQ

How much does generator installation cost in Los Angeles? +

Portable generator outlets cost $500 to $1,000. Manual transfer switches cost $800 to $1,500. Automatic transfer switches cost $1,500 to $3,000. Standby generators (14-22 kW) cost $2,500 to $7,000 installed. Costs depend on generator size, transfer switch type, and gas line requirements. AMY Electric provides firm written quotes after a site visit.

Do I need a permit for a generator installation? +

Yes. Generator installations require an LADBS electrical permit and city inspection. Standby generators also require a gas line permit. AMY Electric files all permits and attends inspections. Permit fees ($200-$450) are included in all-in quotes.

What size generator do I need? +

A typical LA home needs 10-22 kW for whole-home backup. Smaller homes or essential circuits only need 5-10 kW. AMY Electric performs a load calculation to size the right generator for your needs.

Can I run a generator during a power outage? +

Yes. Standby generators auto-start within seconds of an outage. Portable generators require manual connection via a transfer switch. Never connect a generator directly to your panel without a transfer switch — it is dangerous and illegal.

How long does generator installation take? +

Portable generator outlets take 2 to 4 hours. Standby generator installation takes 1 to 2 days including gas line, electrical, and inspection. LADBS inspection follows completion.

Can a standby generator power my whole house? +

Yes, if sized for the whole load. A 14 to 22 kW standby unit runs a typical LA home including AC, EV charger, and kitchen. Smaller units (5 to 10 kW) cover essential circuits only. AMY Electric performs a load calculation to size the right unit.
