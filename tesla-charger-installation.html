FAQ

Tesla Charger FAQ

Do I need a permit for a Tesla charger in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — a permit is required for all new EV charging circuits in LA County and most cities we serve (LA, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena). AMY Electric handles all permitting and inspection. Unpermitted EV charger work can create issues when selling your home.
Can I install a Tesla charger myself? ▾
DIY EV charger installation is risky and often violates local code. Improper wiring, wrong breaker sizing, or missing GFCI protection can create fire hazards. Professional installation ensures safety, code compliance, and warranty validity for both the charger and your home insurance.
Do I need a panel upgrade to install a Tesla Wall Connector? ▾
Not always. A Tesla Wall Connector on a 60A breaker requires sufficient panel capacity. Many newer homes (post-2000) with 200A service have room. Older homes with 100A service may need an upgrade. We include a load calculation in every free estimate.
How long does a Tesla charger installation take? ▾
Most standard installations take 2-4 hours. If a panel upgrade is also needed, expect a full day for the panel (4-8 hours) plus the charger install. We do both in a single visit when possible.
What's the difference between a Tesla Wall Connector and a NEMA 14-50? ▾
The Tesla Wall Connector is a hardwired unit that charges up to 44 miles per hour (60A circuit). A NEMA 14-50 outlet supports up to 30 miles per hour (50A circuit) using the Mobile Connector that comes with your Tesla. The Wall Connector is faster and more convenient for daily use; the NEMA 14-50 is portable and works with any EV.
Do you service all of Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — we install Tesla chargers across Greater LA including Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Burbank, Glendale, Pasadena, Encino, Van Nuys, North Hollywood, and downtown Los Angeles. See our full service area.
