FAQ

Smoke & CO Detector FAQ

Are hardwired smoke detectors required in Los Angeles? ▾
Yes — California State Fire Marshal and LA fire code require hardwired smoke detectors with battery backup in all residential occupancies. Existing battery-only detectors must be upgraded or supplemented with hardwired units during renovations or sales.

How often should smoke detectors be replaced? ▾
Smoke detectors should be replaced every 10 years from the date of manufacture (check the back of the unit). CO detectors typically need replacement every 5-7 years. We check manufacture dates during inspections.

Can I install smoke detectors myself? ▾
While battery-powered detectors are DIY-friendly, hardwired installation requires working with household voltage (120V). Improper wiring, loose connections, or missing interconnect wiring can create safety hazards and fail inspection.

Do smoke detectors need to be interconnected? ▾
Yes — since 1989, smoke detectors in new construction must be interconnected so that when one sounds, all sound. This provides early warning throughout the home, critical for escaping safely.

Where should smoke detectors be placed in my LA home? ▾
On the ceiling or high on the wall (within 12 inches of the ceiling). In bedrooms, outside each sleeping area, and on every level of the home including basements. Not within 3 feet of HVAC supply registers, ceiling fans, or bathrooms with showers.

How long does smoke detector installation take? ▾
Most single detector replacements take 30-60 minutes. Whole-home systems or those requiring new wiring may take 2-4 hours depending on accessibility and number of detectors.
